Who Makes Roborock? Is It Chinese / Xiaomi-Owned?

Short answer: Roborock is a Chinese company — Roborock Technology Co., Ltd. (石头科技), Beijing, listed on the STAR Market as 688169.SH. It was incubated in the Xiaomi ecosystem but is not owned by Xiaomi.

Direct Answer: Roborock's Ownership in One Paragraph

Roborock is a publicly traded Chinese robotics company headquartered in Beijing. Its legal name is Beijing Roborock Century Technology Co., Ltd. (北京石头世纪科技股份有限公司, Chinese brand 石头科技, stock code 688169.SH on the Shanghai STAR Market). It was founded in July 2014 by Chang Jing (昌敬), and its first product — the Xiaomi Mi Robot Vacuum — launched on Xiaomi's Youpin crowdfunding platform in 2016. Xiaomi and Lei Jun's Shunwei Capital remain minority shareholders (~10–15% combined), but Roborock has operated independently since its 2020 IPO and is not a Xiaomi subsidiary.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who makes Roborock vacuums?

Roborock vacuums are designed in Beijing and manufactured in China by Roborock itself. The company has its own ODM/OEM supply chain in Suzhou and Dongguan, rather than being an external rebrand of another manufacturer.

Is Roborock owned by Xiaomi?

No — but it has deep Xiaomi roots. Roborock was incubated inside Xiaomi's ecosystem in 2014 and its first product was the Xiaomi Mi Robot Vacuum, sold through Xiaomi channels. After Roborock launched its own branded products (S5, S6, S7 series) and IPO'd in 2020, it spun out operationally. Xiaomi and Shunwei Capital still hold minority stakes, but Roborock has its own management, R&D, and retail channels.

Where is Roborock from?

Roborock is a Beijing, China company. Its engineering and AI teams are based in Beijing, with manufacturing in Suzhou and Dongguan. It sells in over 100 countries through Amazon, Best Buy, and its own roborock.com.

What country is Roborock made in?

Roborock robot vacuums are made in China. Unlike some consumer electronics brands that have shifted assembly to Vietnam or India, Roborock's production has remained concentrated in mainland China as of 2026.

Is Roborock owned by Ecovacs?

No. Roborock and Ecovacs (科沃斯, 603486.SH) are the two largest Chinese robot-vacuum makers, but they are independent public companies with different headquarters (Beijing vs. Suzhou) and competing product lines.

Is Roborock the same company as Dreame?

No. Dreame Technology (追觅, Suzhou) is a separate high-growth competitor founded by Yu Hao in 2017. Roborock, Ecovacs and Dreame are often compared but are three distinct Chinese companies.

Why This Matters for Buyers

If you are buying a Roborock vacuum in the US or Europe, you are buying a product designed and engineered in Beijing, manufactured in China, and sold through Roborock's own global subsidiary. It is not a Xiaomi house brand, not an Ecovacs rebrand, and not an Indian or European company — even though its customer service and warranty infrastructure in the US and EU are fully local.
