Shao Tianlan

"German engineering + Chinese AI" — a Tsinghua–TU Munich engineer building the eyes and brains of industrial robots.

邵天兰

Founder, Mech-Mind

Founder of Mech-Mind (梅卡曼德) · AI + 3D-vision for industry

Mech-MindIndustrial vision3D visionBeijing

Who he is

Shao Tianlan is a Tsinghua graduate who earned top honours in robotics at TU Munich and spent three and a half years at a German robotics company learning the discipline of industrial reliability. In 2016 he founded Mech-Mind to build AI + 3D-vision "eyes" and "brains" for industrial robots.

The formula — German engineering discipline applied to Chinese AI — has worked. Mech-Mind grew to roughly 22% of the global AI+3D-vision guided component market, with about half of revenue now coming from overseas markets, including Germany.

Why he matters

Shao shows how the industrial side of Chinese robotics — less glamorous than humanoids but deeply profitable — wins on reliability and price. His company is a bridge between Chinese scale and Western engineering standards, and a key test of whether Chinese industrial-robot components can go global.

Key facts

  • 2016: Founded Mech-Mind after 3.5 years at a German robotics company.
  • Market: ~22% of the global AI+3D-vision guided component market.
  • Global: ~50% of revenue from overseas, including Germany.
  • Focus: AI-driven 3D cameras for bin-picking and inspection.
