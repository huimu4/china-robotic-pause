Why this matters

A trillion-yuan shift, still in its first act

From "world's factory" to robot maker

In 2025, domestic brands overtook foreign brands in China's industrial-robot market for the first time — led by Estun, Inovance and SIASUN. The country that once bought robots from ABB and FANUC now produces its own.

Industrial

The humanoid land grab

Over ¥100 billion has poured into humanoid robotics in two years. More than 47 Chinese startups are chasing a machine that can work an eight-hour shift — and the capital is starting to concentrate on a few winners.

Humanoid

A new generation of founders

The average AI-founder age has dropped to 29. China's robotics wave is being led by PhD students, math-olympiad winners and "genius" engineers — many still in their 20s — who are raising hundreds of millions before graduation.
