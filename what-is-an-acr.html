What Is an ACR? Autonomous Case-handling Robots Explained

An ACR (Autonomous Case-handling Robot) is a warehouse robot that rides on top of storage racks and picks individual totes or cartons — a category pioneered by China's Hai Robotics (海柔创新) in 2016.

Direct Answer: What Is an ACR?

An ACR is a wheeled robot that lives on the rack structure itself. It climbs onto the top beam of a storage rack, extends a lift mechanism down to the correct shelf level, and picks or places a single plastic tote (or carton) without human touch. The tote is then brought to a workstation where a human picks the individual SKUs — a goods-to-person workflow with far higher storage density than floor-based AGVs.

The category was invented and standardized by Shenzhen-based Hai Robotics (海柔创新) with its HaiPick system, first shipped in 2017. Today "ACR" is sometimes used generically across Chinese warehouse-robot vendors, but when buyers outside China search "hai robotics acr" they are almost always asking about this rack-traveling tote-picking robot.

How an ACR Works

  1. Rack integration: Standard warehouse racking is modified with a rail/profile on the top beam where the ACR drives.
  2. Tote storage: Plastic totes (usually 500×400×300 mm) are stacked in the rack; multiple SKUs can share a tote.
  3. Robot travel: The ACR drives horizontally on the rack top between aisles, using laser SLAM or markers for navigation.
  4. Vertical lift: A telescoping mast reaches down to pick the target tote (typical 4–10 m rack height).
  5. Transfer to workstation: The tote is carried to a conveyor or lift that drops it to a picking station on the floor; the human picks items and the tote goes back.

Typical ACR Use Cases

  • E-commerce micro-fulfillment: Dense SKU count, fast turnover, limited warehouse footprint.
  • 3PL contract logistics: Mixed-customer totes, rapid reconfiguration.
  • Pharma & healthcare distribution: Controlled-temperature tote storage, traceability.
  • Apparel & footwear: Seasonal SKU churn, high tote count.
  • Automotive spare parts: Thousands of low-volume SKUs in compact racks.

Why Buyers Search "hai robotics acr"

Outside China, ACR is still a little-known category — most logistics engineers still think in terms of Kiva-style shelf-moving AMRs. When a warehouse manager starts researching dense storage automation and types "hai robotics acr" or "acr robot" into Google, they are usually at the very first stage of comparing HaiPick against a traditional AS/RS or a Geek+ shelf-moving system. This article exists to answer that question before they land on a vendor page.
