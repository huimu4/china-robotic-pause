Companies & Startups

More than 47 humanoid startups, a top-three industrial-robot market, and one of the world's most aggressive hardware ecosystems. Here is the landscape, sector by sector.

Humanoid & Embodied AI Industrial Robots Service & Cleaning Aerial & Special Components & Ecosystem
Sector 01

Humanoid & Embodied AI

The most crowded and most funded corner of the industry. China shipped roughly 18,000 humanoids in 2025 — the majority of the global total.

宇
Unitree Robotics 宇树科技
Hangzhou · Founded 2016

The cost-engineering machine. Started with quadruped robots, then became the world's largest humanoid shipper (5,500+ units in 2025; G1 passed 10,000 units). Known for aggressive pricing, full-stack self-developed actuation, and viral demo videos. Filed for a Star Market IPO that cleared in a record 73 days.

Fact: H1 humanoid sprinted 10 m/s in 2026; 2026 target 10k–20k units.
Official site
智
Agibot 智元机器人
Shanghai · Founded 2023

Founded by Huawei "genius" Peng Zhihui and former Huawei VP Deng Taihua. Reached a 15-billion-yuan valuation in three years with 10 financing rounds and 50+ investors. Overtook Unitree in H1 2026 humanoid shipments (~8,400 units, ~44% share) and has launched a Hong Kong IPO.

Fact: "Yuanzheng" (远征) and "Lingxi" (灵犀) product families.
Official site
优
UBTECH 优必选
Shenzhen · Founded 2012

The world's first listed humanoid-robot company (HKEX 09880, Dec 2023). Famous for the Walker series and the very first humanoid robot to "ring the bell" at a stock exchange. 2026 is its "large-scale commercialisation" year: Walker S target raised to 5,000 units, with capacity planned at 10,000.

Fact: Founder Zhou Jian sold his house three times to keep it alive.
Official site
傅
Fourier Intelligence 傅利叶智能
Shanghai · Founded 2015

A rehabilitation-robotics veteran that pivoted into full-size humanoids. Its GR-1 (2023) was among the first mass-produced full-size humanoids; GR-2 and GR-3 followed. Backed by SoftBank Vision Fund 2 and Saudi Aramco's Prosperity7; roughly US$190M raised in total.

Fact: Open-sourced Fourier ActionNet, a large humanoid training dataset.
Official site
众
EngineAI 众擎机器人
Shenzhen · Founded 2023

Founded by Zhao Tongyang (ex-XPeng robotics lead), EngineAI shot to a 10-billion-yuan valuation with its T800 full-size humanoid — the one that kicked its founder across a showroom. Product ladder: T800 (general), SA02 (commercial), PM01 (research). Heading for a Hong Kong IPO.

Fact: US$200M Series B led by Hennan fund and Luxshare in 2026.
Official site
平
AI² Robotics 智平方
Shenzhen · Founded 2023

Founder Guo Yandong's bet on end-to-end VLA (vision-language-action) models. Its "AlphaBot" (爱宝) robots already work in automotive, semiconductor and biopharma plants. Recently valued above 20 billion yuan — reportedly the first embodied-AI unicorn in the Greater Bay Area.

Fact: Full-stack self-developed GOVLA embodied foundation model.
Official site
将
LiberAI 将闲科技
Founded 2025

Tsinghua PhD student Liu Songming's "physical world model" startup. In four months it demoed a two-handed robot peeling bananas, twisting caps and flipping pans — dexterous manipulation widely seen as robotics' "last mile." Nearly 500 million yuan raised in six months from Sequoia China, Meituan, Shunwei and ZhenFund.

Fact: Only Chinese team to close the UMI data loop end-to-end.
加
Booster Robotics 加速进化
Beijing · Founded 2023

Founded by Tsinghua's former "Huoshen" robot-football team captain, Cheng Hao. Positioned around developer ecosystems and education, Booster's T-series humanoids were the standard robot "player" at the 2026 World Humanoid Robot Games and RoboCup. Closed nearly 1 billion yuan in 2026 funding.

Fact: ~92% of teams at the 2026 World Humanoid Games used its platform.
Official site
渊
OriginFlow 渊澈太初
Founded 2025

Not a robot maker — a "data layer" company. Using neuro-muscular (sEMG) signals, OriginFlow captures how human hands perform fine tasks and turns it into robot-training data, with industrial and home-service data deals. Raised 500M+ yuan in five months from Monolith, 58, and others.

Fact: Founder Qin Shentao: 26-year-old Tsinghua PhD, from a remote village.
Sector 02

Industrial Robots

In 2025, domestic brands overtook foreign brands in China's industrial-robot market for the first time. These are the leaders.

新
SIASUN 新松机器人
Shenyang · Founded 2000

China's first robotics company, named after the "Father of Chinese Robotics," Jiang Xinsong. The elder statesman of the industry, spanning industrial robots, mobile robots and — more recently — humanoid research platforms. Listed on the Shenzhen and Hong Kong exchanges.

Fact: Born out of the Shenyang Institute of Automation in 2000.
Official site
埃
Estun 埃斯顿
Nanjing · Founded 1993

The new No.1. In 2025 Estun shipped roughly 33,400 industrial robots (~10.6% share) — the first time a Chinese brand topped the domestic market, overtaking the foreign "big four." Known for vertical integration: 80%+ self-developed content including servo drives and controllers. Listed A-share + H-share (2026).

Fact: No.1 Chinese industrial-robot brand for 8 straight years.
Official site
汇
Inovance 汇川技术
Shenzhen · Listed 2010

The SCARA leader and China's largest motion-control and drive company, born out of Huawei's electrics team. Inovance took a different path from Estun — leveraging its massive servo and PLC base to move up into complete robot arms. Roughly 9–12% share of the domestic industrial-robot market.

Fact: A giant of factory automation beyond just robot arms.
Official site
Sector 03

Service & Cleaning Robots

China is the world's largest market for robot vacuums — and its commercial cleaning robots now mop floors on five continents.

高
Gausium 高仙机器人
Shanghai · Founded 2013

The "boring" winner: 13 years of commercial cleaning robots instead of chasing humanoid hype. Valued at 14 billion yuan, robots deployed across dozens of countries, and a Hong Kong IPO in progress. Launched a new AI cleaning line with SoftBank's US arm in 2026.

Fact: ¥1B+ in same-day orders at its 2026 North America launch.
Official site
云
Narwal 云鲸智能
Dongguan · Founded 2016

Dongguan's robot-vacuum darling, founded by Zhang Junbin (a Li Zexiang/XbotPark protégé). Famous for self-cleaning mops. Exploding overseas: hundreds of millions of yuan in 2026 overseas orders and a strategic partnership with European group Sebo. Valued above 10 billion yuan.

Fact: A flagship of the XbotPark alumni network.
Official site
石
Roborock 石头科技
Beijing · Listed 2020

Founded by Xiaomi-backed engineers (led by Chang Jing), Roborock built robot vacuums into a global consumer brand. Listed on the STAR Market in 2020; consistently among the world's top robot-vacuum makers by revenue.

Fact: One of the most profitable consumer robotics firms in China.
Official site
科
Ecovacs 科沃斯
Suzhou · Founded 1998

The pioneer of Chinese home robotics — started as a vacuum-cleaner OEM in 1998 and pivoted to autonomous floor cleaners under the Deebot brand. Listed in 2018. The company that effectively created China's robot-vacuum category.

Fact: Founded by Qian Dongqi, a former Suzhou tool-factory manager.
Official site
Sector 04

Aerial & Special Robots

Beyond ground robots: the world's drone king, and a new generation of biomimetic flyers.

大
DJI 大疆创新
Shenzhen · Founded 2006

The global drone champion, founded by Wang Tao in a 20-square-metre garage. DJI dominates consumer drones worldwide and defines the reference point for China's hardware ecosystem: a category it created, and a brand the world recognises.

Fact: The proof case for Li Zexiang's "student founder" model.
Official site
鹰
Yingkan Zhiyi 鹰瞰智翼
Shenzhen · Founded 2025

The world's first company focused on embodied-intelligence ornithopter (flapping-wing) robots. Founded by Shanghai Jiao Tong post-00s PhDs led by Chen Hao, with a self-built fluid-simulation engine ("Vortrix") for learning to fly. Three funding rounds in three months.

Fact: Real birds follow its robot birds in flight tests.
Sector 05

Components, Data & the Ecosystem

The "picks and shovels" of the robot boom — and the incubator that produces it.

梅
Mech-Mind 梅卡曼德
Beijing · Founded 2016

AI + 3D-vision "eyes" and "brains" for industrial robots. Founder Shao Tianlan brought German engineering discipline home and paired it with Chinese AI and manufacturing cost advantages. Roughly 22% of the global AI+3D-vision guided component market; ~50% of revenue from overseas.

Fact: Products shipped to ~50 countries; listed in Hong Kong in 2026.
Official site
中
Zhongding Co. 中鼎股份
Anhui · Listed 2006

The rubber-gasket maker turned robot-joint empire. After a decade of European acquisitions (including a French company bought for €1), Zhongding is now a top supplier of harmonic reducers, force sensors and micro-motors for humanoids — with ~300,000 units of reducer orders from EngineAI, Fourier and others.

Fact: 2026 robot business target: ¥320M, doubling to ¥900M in 2027.
Official site
领
Lingyi iTech 领益智造
Shenzhen · Listed 2025 (HK)

The "Apple supply-chain queen" turned robot-hardware maker. After listing in Hong Kong in June 2026 and raising HK$8.3 billion, Lingyi is pouring capital into AI servers, EVs and humanoid-robot joints and skeletons — aiming to be a top-3 global hardware maker in robotics.

Fact: Chairwoman Zeng Fangqin: from a rented workshop to a ¥100B+ market cap.
Official site
天
Tianji Intelligent 天机智能
Dongguan · Founded 2015

Songshan Lake's force-controlled humanoid two-arm specialist — one of the first brands to ship force-controlled dual-arm humanoids in volume, delivering 2,000+ units in four months. Backed in 2026 by Hillhouse, Meituan and Tencent, reaching unicorn status.

Fact: From "world's factory" to robot exporter, made in Dongguan.
Official site
拓
Topstar 拓斯达
Dongguan · Listed 2017

Dongguan's robot-chain "leading enterprise," built on injection-molding automation. Shipped ~12,000 robots in 2025 and launched "Xiaotuo," the first injection-molding-scenario humanoid. Filed for a Hong Kong listing in January 2026.

Fact: 2025 revenue ¥2.51B, back to profit.
Official site
本
Benmo 本末科技
Dongguan · Founded 2018

Direct-drive module specialist with 60%+ domestic share in its niche, plus a line of wheel-legged robots (D1, TITA, "Xingtian") exported to 50+ countries. Partnered with JD for last-mile delivery trials. Headed for a Hong Kong listing.

Fact: A XbotPark ecosystem company from Dongguan.
Official site
X
XbotPark 松山湖机器人基地
Dongguan · Founded 2014

Li Zexiang's hardware startup incubator in Songshan Lake — the "Hogwarts of robotics." More than 80 companies hatched, several unicorns, and DJI as its founding legend. The model now runs in Ningbo, Changzhou, Chongqing, Shenzhen and Hong Kong.

Fact: 6+ unicorns; alumni include DJI, Narwal, and more.
Official site
