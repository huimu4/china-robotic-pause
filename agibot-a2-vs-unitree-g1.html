AgiBot A2 vs Unitree G1: The Two Best-Known Chinese Humanoids, Compared

One is China's most-shipped compact humanoid; the other is the full-size flagship chasing commercial scale. Here is the honest A2 vs G1 comparison for 2026.

By China Robotics Insider · Aug 28, 2026 · Last updated: Aug 28, 2026 · 7 min read
Share on X LinkedIn

Searching for "AgiBot A2 vs Unitree G1" means you have moved past the humanoid hype and are actually comparing machines. Good. These are the two best-known names in Chinese humanoids — and they sit on opposite ends of the market.

The fundamental difference

The A2 and the G1 are not really rivals in the same lane. Unitree's G1 is a ~127 cm, ~35 kg, US$16,000-class compact humanoid engineered for volume — the most-shipped humanoid on the planet. AgiBot's A2 is a full-size 169 cm, ~69 kg flagship aimed at commercial deployment — marketing, guidance, retail, logistics and assembly pilots. One wins on price and reach; the other wins on human scale and service capability.

Why the A2 matters

AgiBot was founded in 2023 by Huawei's former "Genius Youth" engineer Peng Zhihui (稚晖君), and it raised money at astonishing speed. The A2 is the machine that turned that attention into a product line. It holds a Guinness record for continuous walking (106 km in three days), has run over 360 fault-free hours in deployments, and is certified for sale in China, the US and Europe. It is one of the first Chinese humanoids positioned explicitly as a commercial service robot, not a research platform.

Why the G1 matters

The G1 is the opposite bet: make a humanoid cheap enough to ship by the thousands. Its M107 joints, 12-DOF hands and sub-US$20,000 price turned a lab curiosity into the reference hardware for the entire Chinese ecosystem. If you want a humanoid in your lab today, the G1 is the default; that is its superpower.

Which one should you pick?

  • Choose the G1 if: you are a researcher, educator, integrator or demo buyer who wants a capable humanoid now, at the lowest entry price, with the biggest support ecosystem.
  • Choose the A2 if: you are deploying for a real commercial service — marketing, retail, hospitality, logistics or assembly — where full-size human presence and a stronger embodied-AI brain matter more than unit cost.
  • Or neither yet: for heavy manipulation, the middle ground (Fourier GR series, Kepler K2) may fit better than either.

The honest take

These two companies are racing in different weight classes, and both are winning their own race. Unitree is proving humanoids can be cheap; AgiBot is proving they can be commercially deployed at full human scale. Watch the crossover point: the day a full-size, service-grade humanoid approaches G1 pricing is the day this comparison stops being academic.
