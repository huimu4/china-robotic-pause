Jiang Zheyuan (姜哲源)

The 1998-born Tsinghua dropout who founded Noetix Robotics and shipped a humanoid for under 10,000 yuan.

姜哲源

Founder & Chairman, Noetix Robotics (松延动力)

Founder of the N-series humanoid and the "Bumi" consumer robot

NoetixTsinghuaBorn 1998Beijing

Who he is

Jiang Zheyuan (姜哲源, born 1998) is the founder and chairman of Noetix Robotics (松延动力), a Beijing-based humanoid robot maker founded in September 2023. He entered Tsinghua's Department of Electronic Engineering as the 28th-ranked student in Beijing's gaokao, and was pursuing a PhD in reinforcement learning before dropping out to start the company.

Why he matters

Noetix shot to prominence at the first Beijing Humanoid Half-Marathon in 2025, where its metre-tall N2 robot — running at 3.2 m/s — took second and third place. In 2026 the company appeared on the CCTV Spring Festival Gala and launched "Bumi" (小布米), a consumer humanoid priced at 9,998 yuan — one of the cheapest full humanoids on the market. Jiang has repeatedly argued that the future of humanoids is volume, and that a sub-10,000-yuan price point is what unlocks scale. The company has raised close to 200 million RMB across rounds including a Pre-B+ in late 2025.

Key facts

  • 1998: Born.
  • 2023: Dropped out of Tsinghua PhD; founded Noetix Robotics.
  • 2025: N2 finished 2nd/3rd at Beijing Humanoid Half-Marathon; raised Pre-B+ (~200M RMB).
  • 2026: Appeared on CCTV Spring Festival Gala; launched "Bumi" at 9,998 RMB.
