Who he is

Chen Hao is a Shanghai Jiao Tong PhD candidate with four top-journal papers and a team of fellow post-00s. Instead of quadruped or humanoid robots, his company builds ornithopter robots — flapping-wing machines so lifelike that real birds follow them in flight.

His edge is simulation-first engineering. A self-built fluid-simulation engine lets his robot birds "learn" to fly through reinforcement learning before ever touching hardware — collapsing the cost and risk of physical flight testing.

Why he matters

Chen founded the first company focused on embodied-intelligence ornithopter robots, opening a lane most of the industry ignores. Quiet, safe and biomimetic, robot birds point to a future where robots do not just walk or fly — they blend into the natural environment.

Key facts

  • Credentials: Shanghai Jiao Tong PhD candidate, four top-journal papers.
  • Product: Flapping-wing robot birds that real birds follow.
  • Method: RL training in a self-built fluid-simulation engine before hardware.
  • First: First company focused on embodied-intelligence ornithopter robots.
