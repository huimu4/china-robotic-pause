Horizon Robotics

Horizon Robotics (9660.HK) is a Beijing AI-chip leader, expanding its Journey computing platforms from cars to robots.

AI Chip SoC Listed Beijing
地平线

AI chips from cars to robots

Company Overview

  • Horizon was founded by AI scientist Yu Kai and became China's leading autonomous-driving chip company (Journey series).
  • It is extending its computing platforms into robotics, providing the on-device AI compute for perception and control.
  • Horizon is a key computing-supplier play on both autonomous driving and embodied AI.
