Aiper

Aiper (元鼎智能) makes cordless robotic pool cleaners — the Seagull series and more — and is among China's best-selling exported pool robots.

Pool Consumer Export
元鼎智能

Cordless pool cleaners built for the global backyard

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Aiper re-engineered pool cleaning around cordless, battery-powered robots that need no hoses or booster pumps.
  • Its Seagull series and later models became Amazon and retail best-sellers in North America and Europe.
  • Part of a wave of Shenzhen brands (Aiper, Beatbot, Wybot) conquering the global pool-robot category.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Brushless water-jet propulsion with 4WD climbing.
  • Smart navigation with gyro/IMU and multi-mode cleaning.
  • Long-run battery + fast-charge systems.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Vertical supply chain in Shenzhen for motors, seals and batteries.
  • Strong Amazon/retail distribution network overseas.

Competitor Comparison

Head-to-head with Beatbot (also Shenzhen) and US incumbents Dolphin/Maytronics; Aiper leads on cordless and price.
