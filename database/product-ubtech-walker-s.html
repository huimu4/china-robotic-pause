UBTECH Walker S / S1

UBTECH Walker S is the world's first humanoid to work real shifts in real factories — collaborating on automotive assembly lines.

UBTECH Walker S / S1

Official site ↗

Overview

  • Walker S is UBTECH's industrial humanoid, first shown in 2023, with the Walker S1 refining it for factory use.
  • It is one of the first humanoids to complete real shifts on automotive assembly lines, working alongside human workers.
  • UBTECH — the world's first listed humanoid company (HKEX, 2023) — raised its 2026 Walker S target to 5,000 units.

Actuators & Sensors

  • High-torque joint actuators with precise force control.
  • Multi-finger dexterous hands for gripping automotive parts.
  • Vision + LiDAR + IMU perception for human-robot collaboration.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • UBTECH vertically integrates core components including its own servo drives.
  • Harmonic reducers sourced from domestic suppliers including Leaderdrive (Green Harmonic).
  • Deployment backed by partnerships with major Chinese automakers.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Unitree G1, Walker S is a heavier, full-size industrial machine targeting factory shifts rather than research labs. Versus Tesla Optimus, Walker S has already achieved real commercial deployments in Chinese factories.
