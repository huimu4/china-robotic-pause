RoboSense

RoboSense is a Shenzhen LiDAR leader (02498.HK) whose spinning and solid-state sensors equip autonomous vehicles, logistics robots and a growing number of humanoid robots.

LiDAR Sensing Listed Shenzhen
速腾聚创

The LiDAR feeding China's robots

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • RoboSense scaled from automotive LiDAR into a broad sensing platform company, shipping millions of units into ADAS vehicles and a fast-growing robotics segment.
  • Its LiDAR sensors are used in warehouse AMRs, delivery robots, security robots and — increasingly — humanoid robots for 3D environment perception.
  • The company pairs hardware with perception SDKs, making integration into robot stacks straightforward.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Spinning and solid-state LiDAR lines (RS-LiDAR series).
  • Perception SDKs for ROS and embedded platforms.
  • High-volume automotive manufacturing drives cost down for robot applications.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • RoboSense leverages its automotive-scale supply chain to deliver automotive-grade LiDAR at robot-friendly prices.
  • It competes with Hesai and foreign LiDAR makers in China's robot market.
  • Its perception software lowers the barrier for robot makers adopting LiDAR.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Hesai (禾赛): both are China's LiDAR giants; RoboSense has a stronger robotics push, while Hesai is stronger in automotive. In robots, LiDAR competes with vision-only stacks, but RoboSense bets on redundancy.
