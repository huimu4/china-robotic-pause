Inovance Technology

Inovance is China's leading servo and industrial-automation company, and its motors, drives and controllers are foundational components across Chinese robot makers — including emerging humanoid lines.

Servo Industrial Listed Shenzhen
汇川技术

The automation backbone of China's robot industry

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Founded by ex-Huawei engineers, Inovance built China's largest servo and low-voltage inverter business and expanded into complete SCARA and six-axis robots.
  • It supplies servo motors and drives to a wide swath of Chinese industrial and collaborative robot makers, making it a bellwether for the sector.
  • With the humanoid boom, Inovance's high-torque-density motors, drives and motion-control software are candidates for humanoid joint actuation.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Full servo motor + drive + controller product line (MS/MC series etc.).
  • SCARA robots widely used in 3C assembly.
  • Growing portfolio of high-torque hollow-shaft and frameless motors relevant to humanoid joints.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Inovance is primarily a component supplier to robot OEMs rather than a humanoid manufacturer, though it has shown humanoid prototypes.
  • Its scale, quality control and domestic supply chain make it a default choice for Chinese robot makers seeking alternatives to Japanese/Yaskawa components.
  • For humanoids, Inovance competes with other domestic motor makers (Wolong, Maxon-like suppliers) for joint-motor sockets.

Competitor Comparison

Versus foreign servo leaders (Yaskawa, Panasonic, Sanyo): Inovance wins on price, delivery and localization in China. Versus domestic peers (Leadshine, Kinco, Hechuan), Inovance leads in scale and breadth.
