Unitree Go2 Robot Dog

Unitree Go2 is the world's best-selling quadruped robot — a low-cost, high-volume proof that Chinese robotics can ship hardware at scale.

Unitree RoboticsProduct
Unitree Robotics

Unitree Go2 Robot Dog

Official site ↗

Overview

  • The Go2 is Unitree's mass-market quadruped, following the Go1 — the first robot dog to be sold in volume.
  • It became a global phenomenon, rented in fleets of 50+ for inspection, education and entertainment.
  • The heavier B2 industrial variant targets inspection, patrol and logistics.

Specifications

WeightApprox 15 kg (Go2)
PayloadApprox 8-10 kg
SpeedUp to ~5 m/s (running)
BatterySwappable, ~1-2 hours typical
SensorsLiDAR + depth cameras + IMU (Pro)
Release Date2023
PricingStandard from ~US$1,600

Actuators & Sensors

  • High-torque direct-drive style joint modules from Unitree's own line.
  • Robust waterproof / dustproof body for outdoor use.
  • Optional 4G/5G teleop with app control.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Unitree self-supplies its joint motors and controllers.
  • Battery and structural parts from the consumer electronics supply chain in Zhejiang.
  • Volume production is what makes sub-US$2,000 pricing possible.

Competitor Comparison

The Go2 faces competition from Deep Robotics' Lite3 and cheaper hobbyist quads, but dominates on price-performance. The B2 industrial variant competes with Boston Dynamics Spot at a fraction of the price.
