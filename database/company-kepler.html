Kepler Robotics

Kepler Robotics is a Shanghai startup developing the K2 full-size humanoid robot aimed at industrial and commercial tasks.

Humanoid Industrial Shanghai
开普勒机器人

Shanghai's K2 industrial humanoid

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Kepler was founded by serial entrepreneurs with deep robotics and electronics backgrounds.
  • Its K2 humanoid targets factory, logistics and service tasks with full-size bipedal design.
  • The company is part of Shanghai's push to build a humanoid-robot industry cluster.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Full-size humanoid joint modules.
  • Vision + LiDAR perception for navigation and manipulation.
  • Whole-body control for industrial tasks.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Kepler designs core hardware in Shanghai and uses domestic component supply.
  • Part of Shanghai's humanoid-robot innovation ecosystem.
  • Focuses on practical industrial deployment over demo theatrics.

Competitor Comparison

Versus UBTECH and Unitree (full humanoids): Kepler competes on industrial practicality and Shanghai ecosystem ties. Versus UBTECH (Walker), it targets newer, cost-optimized hardware.
