HAI Robotics

HAI Robotics created the autonomous case-handling robot (ACR) category, a denser and cheaper alternative to shelf-carrying AMRs, and has grown into a global warehouse-automation leader.

ACR Logistics Global Shenzhen
海柔创新

Inventor of the ACR warehouse robot

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • HAI Robotics pioneered the ACR concept — robots that fetch individual totes or cases from high-density shelving — instead of moving entire shelves.
  • This approach boosts storage density and throughput while cutting costs, and has been adopted by e-commerce, apparel, retail and 3PL operators worldwide.
  • The company has expanded beyond China into Europe, the US, Japan and Southeast Asia with local support teams.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Self-developed mobile drive and lifting mechanisms for its ACR fleet.
  • Scheduling and warehouse-execution software suite.
  • Vision + LiDAR navigation for precise tote retrieval.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • HAI integrates domestic motors, batteries and sensors, keeping hardware costs competitive against European/US rivals.
  • Software (WES/WCS) is developed in-house, forming the key differentiator.
  • Its vertical stack — hardware, software, deployment — mirrors the model of leading Chinese robotics champions.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Geek+ (AMR): HAI's ACR stores more densely per square meter. Versus Quicktron, HAI has stronger global service footprint; both compete on price with European shelf-carrying systems.
