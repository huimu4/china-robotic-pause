Leju Robotics

Leju Robotics, rooted in Harbin Institute of Technology, builds the Kuavo humanoid robot for education, events and research.

Humanoid Education Harbin
乐聚机器人

Kuavo humanoid for education & R&D

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Leju was founded by Harbin Institute of Technology alumni and built one of China's first commercially available humanoid robots, Kuavo.
  • Kuavo serves education, STEM programs, exhibitions and research institutions.
  • The company bridges academic robotics heritage and commercial productization.

Actuators & Sensors

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Leju leverages Harbin robotics research lineage.
  • Shenzhen base provides manufacturing and cost advantages.
  • Strong presence in Chinese education-robot procurement.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Unitree (G1): Leju's Kuavo focuses more on education and institutional markets, while Unitree targets broader consumer and industrial scale.
