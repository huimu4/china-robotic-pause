Astribot S1

Astribot S1 is a desktop dual-arm robot with 10 m/s end-effector speed — one of the fastest and most dexterous manipulation platforms in the world.

Astribot (星尘智能)Product
Astribot (星尘智能)

Astribot S1

Overview

  • S1 is Astribot's flagship manipulation robot, unveiled in 2024, built on the team's experience from Tencent Robotics X Lab.
  • It demonstrated tasks from folding clothes and writing calligraphy to assembling objects — at human or superhuman speed.
  • The 10 m/s end-effector speed is among the fastest ever shown in a commercial-grade manipulation robot.

Specifications

End-effector SpeedUp to 10 m/s
Degrees of Freedom50+ DOF (dual arms + hands)
PayloadDual-arm manipulation
FormDesktop dual-arm (wheeled base variant)
OriginShenzhen, founded by ex-Tencent Robotics X engineers
Release Date2024
PricingEnterprise / research quotation

Actuators & Sensors

  • High-speed joint actuators enabling 10 m/s end-effector motion.
  • Force-torque sensing for delicate manipulation.
  • Camera + depth perception with learning-based control.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Astribot vertically integrates arm and hand actuation in-house.
  • Shenzhen component ecosystem for motors, reducers and sensors.
  • Part of the broader embodied-AI data flywheel in China.

Competitor Comparison

Versus humanoid platforms, Astribot S1 gives up locomotion for a wheeled, desktop-accessible arm with unmatched speed and dexterity. It competes with a new wave of Chinese 'embodied AI' manipulation robots such as LiberAI's two-arm demo.
