ULS Robotics (傲鲨智能) builds powered exoskeletons for industrial lifting, automotive assembly and logistics work in Shanghai.

Company Overview

  • ULS targets industrial users with exoskeletons that reduce physical strain in repetitive lifting and assembly jobs.
  • Deployed in automotive plants, warehouses and logistics operations across China.
  • A practical, ROI-driven segment of China's exoskeleton market.
