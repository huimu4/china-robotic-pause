PaXini Tech

PaXini Tech builds high-resolution tactile sensors and dexterous hands that let robots 'feel' objects, a critical technology for embodied AI manipulation.

Tactile Sensor Dexterous Hand Shenzhen
帕西尼感知

Giving robots the sense of touch

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • PaXini specializes in high-resolution tactile (touch) sensors that give robot fingertips pressure, slip and texture sensing.
  • Its sensors and hands enable delicate manipulation — handling eggs, fruit, cables and soft objects.
  • The company serves humanoid research, industrial manipulation and embodied-AI development.

Actuators & Sensors

  • High-density tactile sensor arrays for fingertips.
  • Multi-finger dexterous hands with integrated sensing.
  • Force and slip detection for safe grasping.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • PaXini designs its own sensor ASICs and manufacturing.
  • Shenzhen ecosystem for electronics and assembly.
  • Tactile sensing is a key differentiator vs vision-only stacks.

Competitor Comparison

Versus international tactile leaders: PaXini offers localization and cost advantages. In China, it competes with a handful of tactile startups, leading in resolution and integration.
