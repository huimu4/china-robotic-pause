MicroPort MedBot

MicroPort MedBot (2252.HK) is China's leading surgical-robot company, best known for the Toumai laparoscopic system competing with da Vinci.

Surgical Medical Listed Shanghai

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • MicroPort MedBot is part of the MicroPort medical-device group and the leading domestic challenger to Intuitive Surgical's da Vinci.
  • Its Toumai laparoscopic robot has been approved and deployed in Chinese hospitals for urology, general surgery and gynecology.
  • The company is at the center of China's push for affordable domestic surgical robots.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Articulated surgical arms with high precision.
  • 3D high-definition vision system.
  • Force feedback and tremor filtering.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • MedBot develops surgical robots under strict medical-device regulation.
  • Manufacturing in Shanghai with domestic component supply.
  • Backed by MicroPort group's extensive medical network.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Intuitive Surgical (da Vinci): MedBot wins on price and localization in China, with an expanding approved-surgery base. Verses domestic peers (TINAVI, Edge), it leads in laparoscopic surgery.
