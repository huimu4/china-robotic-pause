Noetix Robotics (松延动力)

The Beijing humanoid startup built by a 1998-born Tsinghua dropout — known for the racing N2 and a 9,998 RMB consumer humanoid.

Company Overview

  • Noetix Robotics (北京松延动力) was founded in September 2023 by Jiang Zheyuan, a Tsinghua PhD student who dropped out to focus on reinforcement-learning-driven humanoid control.
  • The company made its name with the metre-tall N2, which finished second and third at the 2025 Beijing Humanoid Half-Marathon running at 3.2 m/s — beating several larger, more expensive full-size humanoids.
  • In late 2025 Noetix launched "Bumi" (小布米), a consumer humanoid priced at 9,998 RMB (~US$1,400) — one of the cheapest full humanoids on the global market.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Compact, high-torque-density joints sized for a child-sized form factor, optimized for running dynamics rather than heavy payload.
  • In-house reinforcement-learning motion controller trained largely in simulation.
  • Modular design to keep Bumi's bill of materials under 10,000 RMB.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Noetix leans on the Shenzhen domestic actuator ecosystem — frameless torque motors, harmonic reducers and low-cost IMUs — to hit consumer price points.
  • The founder has publicly argued that cost engineering, not just algorithm, is the bottleneck to humanoid scale, and that a sub-10,000 RMB price point is what unlocks volume.

Competitor Comparison

Versus full-size humanoids (Unitree G1, AgiBot, Fourier): Noetix deliberately builds smaller, cheaper, child-sized robots and targets the consumer/education market rather than factory floors. This trades payload and dexterity for price and motion liveliness — directly comparable to Unitree's G1 but aimed at a different buyer.
