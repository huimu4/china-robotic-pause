ZW Motion (Zhaowei)

ZW Motion is a Shenzhen precision-motion company that built its micro gearbox business and is now a leading domestic maker of dexterous hands for humanoids.

Micro Drive Dexterous Hand Listed Shenzhen
兆威机电

Micro drives & dexterous hand technology

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • ZW Motion built a strong business in micro planetary gearboxes and precision drives for consumer electronics and automobiles.
  • It leveraged this to develop dexterous robot hands with multiple fingers and high degrees of freedom.
  • Its hands are used in humanoid robot research and prototypes across China.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Micro planetary gearboxes and high-torque miniature motors.
  • Multi-finger dexterous hands with tactile options.
  • Customizable finger and wrist joint modules.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • ZW Motion vertically integrates micro gear design and manufacturing.
  • Strong automotive/consumer volume gives it cost and quality advantages.
  • Dexterous hands target the humanoid boom.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Inspire Robots (dexterous hands): ZW Motion leverages its micro-gear industrial base for scale; Inspire specializes in servo actuators. Both compete in China's humanoid hand market.
