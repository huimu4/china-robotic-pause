Astribot

Astribot shocked the robotics world with its S1 humanoid arm, performing ultra-fast, human-speed manipulation tasks.

Humanoid Dexterous Shenzhen
星尘智能

Ultra-fast dexterous manipulation

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Astribot's S1 demo videos went viral for showing a robot arm performing tasks at human speed — pouring, writing, folding and handling objects.
  • The company focuses on embodied-AI-driven manipulation with high-speed, dexterous control.
  • Astribot represents China's push to match or exceed human-level manipulation speed.

Key Facts

Actuators & Sensors

  • High-speed, high-precision arm actuation.
  • Dexterous end-effectors for fine manipulation.
  • Vision-language-action (VLA) control pipeline.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Astribot leverages Shenzhen's hardware ecosystem for motors and sensors.
  • Core differentiator is its embodied-AI software and control speed.
  • Strong venture backing funds rapid iteration.

Competitor Comparison

Versus AgiBot and Unitree (full humanoids): Astribot focuses on manipulation speed and arm-centric design. Versus foreign embodied-AI rivals, it competes on China-first hardware cost.
