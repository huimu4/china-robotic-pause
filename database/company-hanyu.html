Hanyu Group (汉宇集团)

The Jiangmen appliance-pump company whose 36%-owned Tongchuan Technology supplies harmonic reducers and joint modules to XPeng's IRON humanoid.

Company Overview

  • Hanyu Group (汉宇集团, SZSE: 300403) is a Jiangmen-based manufacturer historically known for washing-machine drain pumps, which has diversified into industrial robots, NEV components and charging piles.
  • Its robot exposure runs through Tongchuan Technology (同川科技), in which Hanyu holds a 36.02% stake. Tongchuan makes harmonic reducers, electromechanical integrated actuators and robot joint modules.
  • Tongchuan is reported to supply waist-joint harmonic reducers and electromechanical joint modules to XPeng's IRON humanoid, with an estimated 2025 order book of over RMB 500M.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Tongchuan's harmonic reducers target the industrial-robot and humanoid-joint market, competing with Green Harmonic (Leaderdrive), Laifual and Japanese Harmonic Drive.
  • Products span harmonic reducers, electromechanical integrated actuators and robot joint modules used in robots, CNC rotary tables and new-energy equipment.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Green Harmonic and Laifual: Tongchuan is smaller and vertically aligned to a single lead customer (XPeng), while Hanyu's listed-company balance sheet gives it capacity to scale. Versus Sumitomo/HD System: it competes on price in the domestic humanoid ramp rather than on premium industrial precision.
