GSK CNC

GSK CNC (广州数控) is China's largest domestic CNC-system maker, using that base to build industrial robots for machine tending and handling.

CNC Industrial Guangzhou
广州数控

CNC leader that also makes robots

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • GSK is a pillar of China's machine-tool industry, supplying CNC systems, servos and drives nationwide.
  • Its industrial robots (6-axis and jointed arms) automate machine tending, loading and handling in factories.
  • A rare full-stack Chinese player spanning control, drive and robot.
