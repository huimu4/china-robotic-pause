StarChart Robotics is a Beijing startup building dexterous robot arms and embodied-AI models for general manipulation tasks.

Company Overview

  • StarChart Robotics builds dexterous, low-cost robot arms paired with embodied-AI models for general manipulation.
  • Its approach combines hardware accessibility with software intelligence for broad task coverage.
  • The startup is part of Beijing's wave of embodied-AI companies.
