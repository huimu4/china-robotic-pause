Googol Technology

Googol Technology (301510.SZ) is a Shenzhen motion-controller maker founded by HKUST professors, serving robot and machine builders.

Motion Controller Listed Shenzhen
固高科技

Motion controllers from HKUST roots

Company Overview

  • Googol was founded by HKUST (Hong Kong University of Science and Technology) professors and became the backbone of Chinese motion-control education and industry.
  • Its controllers run in robots, CNC machines, 3D printers and countless machines across China.
  • Googol's academic roots make it a key talent source for the robotics industry.

Competitor Comparison
