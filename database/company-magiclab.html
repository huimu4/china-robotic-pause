MagicLab

MagicLab is a Shanghai startup building the Wonder humanoid robot, emphasizing dual dexterous hands and embodied AI.

Humanoid Dexterous Shanghai
魔法原子

Wonder humanoid with dexterous hands

Company Overview

  • MagicLab is developing the Wonder humanoid robot with a focus on bimanual dexterity and task-capable hands.
  • The startup targets home and service scenarios where manipulation matters.
  • It is part of the Shanghai humanoid-robot startup cluster.
