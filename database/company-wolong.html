Wolong Electric

Wolong Electric (600580.SH) is a Shaoxing motor and drive leader expanding into robot actuators and electric-drive systems.

Motor Drive Listed Shaoxing
卧龙电驱

Motors & drives for robotics

Company Overview

  • Wolong is one of China's largest motor manufacturers, with a global business in industrial and EV motors.
  • It is developing robot drive systems and joint actuators for the humanoid market.
  • Scale in conventional motors gives Wolong cost and manufacturing advantages.
