Wuzhou Xinchun (五洲新春)

The Zhejiang bearing maker converting precision-machining know-how into 2.1M sets/year of planetary roller screws and robot bearings for humanoid OEMs.

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Wuzhou Xinchun (浙江五洲新春集团, SH: 603667) is a Zhejiang manufacturer historically known for ball bearings, auto parts and thermal-management components.
  • In March 2025 it signed a strategic framework with Hangzhou Xinjian Transmission (杭州新剑) to co-produce planetary roller screws, miniature ball screws and their roller/nut components for humanoid robots.
  • It is building a dedicated humanoid-components project with planned capacity of 2.1M sets/year of planetary roller screws and 150k sets/year of robot bearings; humanoid customers disclosed include Zhejiang-based OEMs, XPeng and Xinjian itself.
