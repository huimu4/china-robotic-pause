Company Overview

  • Robot Era (北京星动纪元) was founded in August 2023 and is the only humanoid company directly backed by Tsinghua University. Its founder Chen Jianyu is an assistant professor and PhD supervisor at Tsinghua's Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS, "Yao Class").
  • Chen earned his bachelor's degree from Tsinghua's Department of Precision Instruments and his PhD at UC Berkeley under Masayoshi Tomizuka (US National Academy of Engineering, MPC pioneer), before being recruited by Turing Award laureate Andrew Yao to Tsinghua.
  • Robot Era pursues a full-stack, vertically integrated route — "universal body + universal brain" — with commercial deployments in logistics and industrial settings. In 2026 its Ctrl-World world model, built with Stanford's Chelsea Finn team, topped the World Arena embodied benchmark.
