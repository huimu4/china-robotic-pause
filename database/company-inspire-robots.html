Inspire Robots

Inspire Robots makes miniature high-torque servo actuators and dexterous hands that are widely used by Chinese humanoid startups, education programs and research labs.

Dexterous Hand Servo Components Beijing
因时机器人

The hands behind China's humanoids

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Inspire Robots specializes in the smallest precision servo actuators and multi-finger dexterous hands in China, filling a gap that was long dominated by foreign component makers.
  • Its actuators and hands are used by multiple Chinese humanoid robot developers for manipulation, as well as in education and research.
  • The company's high-precision, low-cost actuators help domestic humanoids keep bills of materials local.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Micro high-torque servo actuators with integrated control electronics.
  • Multi-DOF dexterous hands with tactile-capable fingertips.
  • Customizable joint modules for humanoid wrists, fingers and toes.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Inspire Robots vertically integrates motor, gearhead and driver design in Beijing.
  • Its components directly substitute for imported Japanese/US dexterous-hand actuators at lower cost.
  • As humanoids scale, Inspire is positioned to be a core component supplier to multiple OEMs simultaneously.

Competitor Comparison

Versus imported dexterous hands (e.g., from Shadow Robot): Inspire offers far lower cost with localization. Versus domestic peers (Linkerbot, Wuji, 灵巧智能), Inspire is established with the broadest installed base.
