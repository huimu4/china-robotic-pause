Hengli Hydraulic

Hengli Hydraulic (601100.SH) is a Wuxi precision-manufacturing giant entering humanoid roller-screw and linear-actuator supply.

Company Overview

  • Hengli is a global leader in hydraulic cylinders and precision machining, serving construction, excavators and industrial markets.
  • Its precision machining capabilities position it to make planetary roller screws and linear actuators for humanoid robots.
  • Hengli is among the most-watched Chinese incumbents in the humanoid supply chain.
