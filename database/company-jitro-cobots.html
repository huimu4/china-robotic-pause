Frequently Asked Questions

What does JITRI Cobots do?

JITRI Cobots (集萃智造) is a Chinese maker of collaborative robots. Its focus is Collaborative robots, serving manufacturing and education customers that need safe, easy-to-deploy 6-axis arms.

How does JITRI Cobots compare with Universal Robots or Doosan?

Chinese cobot makers such as JITRI Cobots compete with Universal Robots and Doosan on price, local support and fast iteration. JITRI Cobots's edge comes from Collaborative robots, at a cost structure global brands often cannot match.

Where is JITRI Cobots based?

JITRI Cobots (集萃智造) is headquartered in Nanjing, China, China.
