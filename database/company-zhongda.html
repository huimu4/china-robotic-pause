Zhongda Leader

Zhongda Leader is a Ningbo precision-reducer maker (002896.SZ) supplying planetary and RV gearboxes to Chinese robot and automation builders.

Company Overview

  • Zhongda Leader builds precision planetary and RV reducers widely used in robots, CNC and automation equipment.
  • Its reducers serve industrial robots, AGVs and growing humanoid-robot joint demand.
  • The company is a key part of China's localization of precision robot components.
