Company Overview

  • Northern Precision (上海北特科技, SH: 603009) is a Shanghai Jiading-based auto-parts maker (steering shafts) that has pivoted heavily into humanoid actuator screws.
  • In 2024 it set up Shanghai Beite Robotics and announced an RMB 1.85B planetary roller screw R&D and production base in Kunsan (140 mu).
  • It is reportedly a tier-2 supplier of hand screws to Tesla's Optimus program, and develops planetary roller screws, ball screws and trapezoidal screws for humanoid limb joints and dexterous hands.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Wuzhou Xinchun and Best: Beite is the most direct Tesla-Optimus-linked Chinese screw maker, with a dedicated Kunsan plant and a Thailand line. Versus Schaeffler/Rexroth: it competes on cost and proximity to Chinese and Tesla's Shanghai-built supply chain, but is still in the sampling/scaling phase.
