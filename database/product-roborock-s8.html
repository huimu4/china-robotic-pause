Roborock S8

Roborock S8 is a flagship robot vacuum from the company that turned hardware specs into a global home-robotics brand.

Overview

  • Roborock started as a Xiaomi ecosystem company in 2014 and became an independent global brand, known for best-in-class hardware.
  • The S8 series pairs dual rubber brushes, strong suction and self-emptying docks with advanced obstacle avoidance.
  • Roborock consistently ranks among the highest-selling robot vacuums in Europe and North America.

Specifications

CategoryRobot vacuum & mop
SuctionUp to 8000 Pa class (flagship)
BrushesDual counter-rotating rubber brushes
DockSelf-emptying + self-washing mop dock
Obstacle Avoidance3D structured light + RGB camera
PricingFrom ~US$400

Actuators & Sensors

  • High-efficiency brushless motors with strong suction.
  • VibraRise / spinning mop modules with lifting.
  • LiDAR + 3D structured light navigation.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Roborock designs core components in-house, from motors to the docking station.
  • Manufacturing and supply chain in Shenzhen / China's consumer electronics cluster.
  • App and software stack developed in-house in Beijing.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Ecovacs, Roborock is the hardware-specs challenger with stronger suction and brushes per dollar; Ecovacs counters with a broader line and earlier brand presence. Both dominate the global premium segment.
