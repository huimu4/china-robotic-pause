Pudu Robotics

Pudu Robotics is the global leader in restaurant delivery robots, deploying tens of thousands of BellaBot and other robots across restaurants worldwide.

Delivery Service Global Shenzhen
普渡科技

World's leading restaurant delivery robots

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Pudu built the world's most-deployed fleet of restaurant delivery robots, shipping tens of thousands of units across China, Asia, Europe and the US.
  • Its robot family covers food delivery, greeting, cleaning and advertising robots for hospitality and retail.
  • Pudu is a flagship example of Chinese service robotics going global at scale.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Self-developed drive, navigation and scheduling systems.
  • Vision + LiDAR navigation for crowded restaurant environments.
  • Cloud fleet management and remote monitoring.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Pudu integrates Shenzhen supply chains for motors, sensors and batteries.
  • Software-defined fleet management is its core moat.
  • Global localization teams support overseas deployments.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Keenon (delivery robots): Pudu leads in global restaurant deployment. Versus Bear Robotics and foreign rivals, Pudu wins on price, volume and feature velocity.
