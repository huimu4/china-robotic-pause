SongYan Dynamics

SongYan Dynamics is a Beijing startup famous for its fast-running humanoid robots and bionic legged machines.

Humanoid Bionic Beijing
松延动力

Fast-running bipedal robots

Company Overview

  • SongYan Dynamics drew global attention with videos of its humanoid running at impressive speed and stability.
  • Its bionic robots explore agile locomotion — running, jumping and dynamic balance.
  • The startup focuses on dynamic motion control as its core competency.

Actuators & Sensors

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Venture funding supports aggressive development.
