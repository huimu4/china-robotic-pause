Double-Ring Transmission

Double-Ring Transmission is a major Chinese maker of RV reducers and planetary roller screws, key components for industrial and humanoid robot joints.

Reducer RV Screw Listed
双环传动

China's RV reducer & screw specialist

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Double-Ring started in automotive gears and expanded into robot reducers, becoming China's primary domestic RV reducer maker.
  • Its RV-E series serves heavy-load industrial robot joints, while lightweight versions target humanoids.
  • Planetary roller screws position it for linear-actuator-style humanoid joints.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Double-Ring masters whole-process gear machining, heat treatment and assembly.
  • High-strength steel sourced domestically.
  • Competes with Japanese Nabtesco on price in China.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Nabtesco (Japan): Double-Ring wins on localization and price. Versus domestic peers, it leads in RV reducer scale and has a growing humanoid socket.
