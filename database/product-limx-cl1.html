LimX Dynamics CL-1

Overview

  • CL-1 is LimX's full-size humanoid, announced in 2024, developed from the company's quadruped and wheeled-leg robots.
  • LimX is known for aggressive gait engineering — its humanoid has demonstrated dynamic walking and stair climbing.
  • The company, founded by ex-ETH and ex-Carnegie Mellon robotics engineers, targets whole-body locomotion research and industrial roles.

Specifications

HeightApprox 170 cm
WeightApprox 55 kg
Degrees of FreedomFull-body humanoid DOF
MobilityDynamic biped walking, stair climbing
OriginSpin-off of quadruped platform
Release Date2024
PricingResearch / enterprise quotation

Actuators & Sensors

  • High-power density actuators inherited from LimX's quadruped line.
  • Force-sensitive whole-body control for dynamic balance.
  • Vision + IMU perception stack.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • LimX self-develops key locomotion algorithms and actuator control.
  • Shenzhen-based, drawing on the local humanoid component cluster.
  • Reducers from domestic suppliers including Leaderdrive.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Unitree G1, CL-1 is a full-size machine emphasizing locomotion research and industrial trials rather than low-cost volume. It competes with Kepler K2 and EngineAI T800 in the full-size segment.
