Unitree Robotics

Unitree Robotics turned cost-engineered quadruped robots into global best-sellers and then into one of the world's most-shipped humanoid robot lines.

Humanoid Quadruped Go2 Hangzhou
宇树科技

World's best-selling quadruped & humanoid robot maker

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Unitree started in 2016 building affordable quadruped robots, and its Go-series robot dogs became the world's best-selling consumer and research legged robots.
  • In 2023-2025 it pushed into full-size humanoids (H1, G1, G2), leveraging its vertical actuator design and cost discipline to undercut global rivals.
  • Unitree humanoids are now deployed in research labs, universities and early commercial pilots worldwide.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Self-developed high-torque joint actuators and drives in-house.
  • Proprietary motion-control stack for walking, running and whole-body balance.
  • Depth cameras, LiDAR options, IMU and joint torque sensing.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Unitree vertically integrates actuator design and manufacturing in Hangzhou.
  • Harmonic reducers sourced from domestic suppliers including Leaderdrive (Green Harmonic).
  • Its scale in quadruped production gives it component-cost advantages for humanoid lines.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Boston Dynamics: Unitree wins on price and shipping volume; Boston Dynamics leads in research-grade acrobatics. Verses domestic peers (AgiBot, Fourier), Unitree leads in total legged-robot volume and brand reach.
