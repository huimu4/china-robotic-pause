Elite Robots

Elite Robots (艾利特机器人) makes collaborative robot arms — the EC and CS series — that help small manufacturers automate safely.

Cobot 6-axis Suzhou
艾利特机器人

Collaborative arms for SMEs worldwide

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Elite Robots focuses on safe, easy-to-deploy 6-axis collaborative arms for small and mid-size manufacturers.
  • Its EC series and lighter CS series are widely used in pick-and-place, assembly, inspection and machine tending.
  • One of China's most exported cobot brands.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Integrated joint modules with torque sensing.
  • 6-DOF collaborative arms up to ~16kg payload.
  • Force control & safety-rated stops.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Suzhou manufacturing with domestic joint/actuator supply.
  • Global distributor network and integration partners.

Competitor Comparison

Competes head-to-head with JAKA, Dobot, AUBO and global leader Universal Robots on price and ease-of-use.
