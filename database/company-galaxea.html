Galaxea AI

Galaxea AI (星海图) is a Beijing startup coupling embodied foundation models with wheeled dual-arm robots (R1 series) for physical AI.

Company Overview

  • Galaxea builds full-stack embodied AI: foundation models for manipulation plus the robots to run them.
  • Its wheeled dual-arm R1 platform targets household and light-manufacturing tasks.
  • Represents the new wave of Chinese 'physical AI' startups.
