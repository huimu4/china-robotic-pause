Unitree G1 Humanoid Robot

Unitree G1 is the compact, cost-engineered humanoid that became the world's best-selling humanoid robot — built for research labs, developers and early commercial use.

Unitree G1 Humanoid Robot

Official site ↗

Overview

  • Unitree G1 is Unitree's mass-oriented humanoid platform, targeting research laboratories, enterprise customers and embodied-AI developers.
  • It inherits actuator technology from Unitree's quadruped robots and supports whole-body motion control and dexterous manipulation.
  • Positioned as a low-cost alternative to full-size humanoids, G1 is the most-shipped humanoid robot line in the world.

Specifications

Degrees of Freedom23 in body + 12-DOF three-finger hands
HeightApprox 127 cm (4 ft 2 in)
WeightApprox 35 kg (77 lb)
Walking SpeedUp to ~2 m/s
JointsUnitree M107 joint motors, high torque density
Sensors3D LiDAR, depth cameras, IMU
BatterySwappable, ~2-hour typical operation
Release Date2024
PricingFrom ~US$16,000 (education/reference config)

Actuators & Sensors

  • Unitree M107 high-torque joint motors with field-serviceable modular design.
  • Force-torque sensing at key joints for whole-body control.
  • 3D LiDAR + depth cameras + IMU for balance and perception.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Core joint motors and drive units are self-developed by Unitree.
  • Key reducer components sourced from domestic Chinese suppliers including Leaderdrive (Green Harmonic).
  • Battery and compute follow consumer-electronics cost discipline — the core of G1's pricing advantage.

Competitor Comparison

Compared with Tesla Optimus and Figure-01, Unitree G1 focuses on cost, agility and research-friendly openness rather than full-size factory automation. It competes with Kepler K2 and EngineAI's smaller models at the budget end of the market.
