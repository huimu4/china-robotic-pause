VisionNav

VisionNav builds vision-navigation autonomous forklifts and mobile robots that move heavy loads in warehouses, ports and factories.

Forklift AGV Vision Shenzhen
未来机器人

Vision-guided autonomous forklifts

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • VisionNav uses vision-based navigation (without floor markers) to power autonomous forklifts and heavy-load mobile robots.
  • Its solutions serve warehouses, 3PL, automotive and port logistics with high-precision material handling.
  • The company has expanded internationally across Asia, Europe and the Americas.

Key Facts

Full NameShenzhen VisionNav Robotics

Founded2016

Core ProductsAutonomous forklifts, tuggers, mobile robots
StatusLeading Chinese autonomous forklift maker

Actuators & Sensors

  • Vision + LiDAR navigation stack for high-precision positioning.
  • Heavy-load drive and lift mechanisms.
  • Fleet scheduling and warehouse-execution software.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • VisionNav integrates domestic motors, batteries and sensors.
  • Its software navigation stack is the key moat.
  • Competitive cost against European and Japanese AGV incumbents.

Competitor Comparison

Versus European AGV makers (KION, Toyota): VisionNav wins on price and vision-based flexibility. Versus domestic peers, it is a category leader in vision-navigation forklifts.
