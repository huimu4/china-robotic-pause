SIASUN Robot

SIASUN is China's first and largest state-linked robot company, born from the Shenyang Institute of Automation, spanning industrial arms, mobile robots and smart manufacturing lines.

Industrial Mobile Listed Shenyang
新松机器人

China's state-backed robotics flagship

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • SIASUN traces its roots to the Chinese Academy of Sciences' Shenyang Institute of Automation, making it the founding institution of China's modern robot industry.
  • It offers industrial arms, AGV/mobile robots and complete smart-manufacturing solutions, deploying across automotive, semiconductor and new-energy factories.
  • As the humanoid wave rises, SIASUN has shown its own humanoid R&D and continues to anchor China's state-led robotics strategy.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Self-developed robot controllers and drives across its product families.
  • Heavy-duty industrial arms for welding and handling, plus a wide AGV line.
  • Integrated vision and scheduling software for factory automation.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • SIASUN leverages its CAS roots and state relationships for large SOE and government projects.
  • Reducers are sourced from domestic suppliers as well as global brands depending on project grade.
  • Its broad portfolio (arm + mobile + systems) makes it a systems integrator as much as a robot maker.

Competitor Comparison

Versus ESTUN: SIASUN is more diversified into mobile robots and complete lines, while ESTUN is stronger in standard industrial robots and servos. SIASUN's institutional and government pipeline is a unique moat.
