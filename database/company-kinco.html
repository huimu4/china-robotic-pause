Kinco Automation

Kinco Automation (688160.SH) is a Shanghai maker of servo and stepper drives used in industrial, collaborative and mobile robots.

Company Overview

  • Kinco builds cost-competitive servo and stepper drive systems widely used in Chinese automation and robotics.
  • Its drives serve cobots, AGVs, packaging and general machinery.
  • Kinco is a key domestic alternative to Japanese and European motion brands.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Kinco integrates drive electronics design and production in Shanghai.
  • Domestic supply chain for low-cost, high-volume production.
  • Broad distribution to Chinese machine builders and integrators.

Competitor Comparison
