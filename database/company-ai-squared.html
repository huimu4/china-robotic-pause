AI² Robotics is a Beijing startup building general-purpose embodied intelligence to control mobile manipulation robots in the real world.

Company Overview

  • AI² Robotics builds a general-purpose embodied-AI brain that lets robots perceive, reason and act in real environments.
  • Its technology targets mobile manipulation — robots that move and handle objects in homes and workplaces.
  • The startup represents the software-first camp of China's embodied-AI wave.
