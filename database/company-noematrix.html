Noematrix

Noematrix is a Shanghai embodied-AI startup, linked to Fourier Intelligence, building general robot intelligence for real-world manipulation.

Embodied-AI brain for manipulation

Company Overview

  • Noematrix was founded by alumni of Fourier Intelligence, a leading humanoid and rehab-robot maker.
  • It builds a general manipulation brain that can control different robot arms and humanoids.
  • The startup targets home and industrial task execution with vision-language-action models.
