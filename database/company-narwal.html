Narwal

Narwal invented the self-washing robot mop and is now betting on humanoid home robots as its next act.

Home Mopping Startup Shenzhen
云鲸智能

The self-cleaning mopping robot pioneer

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Narwal pioneered the self-cleaning base for robot mops, a now-standard feature, and built a premium home-robot brand.
  • It has expanded into humanoid home-robot R&D, betting that general-purpose humanoids will enter households.
  • The company is one of China's most prominent consumer-robot startups with strong VC backing.

Key Facts

Full NameNarwal Robotics (Shenzhen)
Chinese Name云鲸智能（深圳）
Founded2016
HeadquartersShenzhen, China
FounderZhang Junbin (张峻彬)
Core ProductsFreo mopping/vacuum robots, humanoid home prototypes
StatusHigh-growth home robot startup

Actuators & Sensors

  • Self-developed mop-cleaning docks and navigation.
  • Advanced mopping pressure and auto-drying systems.
  • Humanoid prototypes with dual arms for home tasks.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Narwal integrates Shenzhen supply chains for motors, sensors and batteries.
  • Its software and dock design are key differentiators.
  • Funding supports long-horizon humanoid home-robot R&D.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Roborock and Ecovacs: Narwal leads in mopping-first design and self-cleaning innovation, but trails in overall vacuum market share.
