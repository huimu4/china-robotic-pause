AgiBot A2 (Yuanzheng) Humanoid Robot

AgiBot A2, codenamed Yuanzheng (远征), is the second-generation full-size humanoid from AgiBot with self-developed dexterous hands.

Official site ↗

Overview

  • AgiBot A2 is AgiBot's second-generation full-size humanoid platform, targeting industrial inspection, factory logistics and embodied-AI research.
  • It is notable for its self-developed dexterous hand with high degrees of freedom, enabling fine manipulation tasks most competing humanoids cannot perform.
  • The company, founded by former Huawei genius-scholar Peng Zhihui, shipped ~8,400 humanoids in H1 2026 — the most in the world.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Self-developed high-torque rotary joint actuators for lower body.
  • Integrated linear actuators for upper body and arm joints.
  • Self-developed multi-finger dexterous hand with tactile sensing.
  • Depth camera + LiDAR + IMU + joint torque sensors.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • AgiBot vertically integrates key actuators and dexterous-hand production in-house.
  • Reducer components sourced from domestic suppliers including Leaderdrive (Green Harmonic).
  • Battery packs use domestic lithium-ion cells; compute uses Chinese AI chip solutions.

Competitor Comparison

Compared with Unitree G1, AgiBot A2 is taller, heavier and carries more DOF — particularly in the hands — making it stronger at manipulation. Versus Tesla Optimus, A2 is already in limited commercial deployment in Chinese factories.
