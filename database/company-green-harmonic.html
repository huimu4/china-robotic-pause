Leaderdrive (Green Harmonic)

Leaderdrive, known as Green Harmonic (绿的谐波), is China's leading harmonic reducer manufacturer and a critical supplier to humanoid robot joints.

Reducer Harmonic Listed Suzhou

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Leaderdrive broke the Japanese duopoly in harmonic reducers and became China's first listed harmonic reducer manufacturer.
  • Its reducers are used in industrial robots, cobots and increasingly humanoid robot joints.
  • As humanoids scale, each unit requires 14-28+ harmonic reducers, making Leaderdrive a prime beneficiary.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Y-series and lightweight YC-series harmonic reducers.
  • Integrated reducer + motor + drive joint modules.
  • High precision and repeatability for robot joints.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Leaderdrive masters the flexspline heat-treatment and machining bottleneck in-house.
  • Raw materials sourced from domestic specialty steel mills.
  • Expanding capacity to meet humanoid demand.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Japanese Harmonic Drive Systems: Leaderdrive wins on price and localization in China. Versus domestic peers (Hengtong, Tongchuan), Leaderdrive leads in scale and qualification.
