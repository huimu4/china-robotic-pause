Qinchuan Machine Tool

Qinchuan Machine Tool (000837.SZ) is a Baoji company supplying RV reducers and precision machine tools to Chinese robot makers.

Reducer RV Listed Baoji
秦川机床

RV reducers & precision machine tools

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Qinchuan is one of China's oldest and most important machine-tool makers, with deep gear-manufacturing expertise.
  • Its RV reducers serve industrial robots and are now targeted at humanoid joints.
  • Qinchuan's own machine tools give it vertical integration for precision gear production.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Qinchuan's machine-tool business supplies the gear-cutting and grinding machines used in its own reducers.
  • Shaanxi industrial base and state-linked support.
  • Long history of supplying Chinese industrial-robot makers.
