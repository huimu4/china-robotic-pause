Tuopu Group

Tuopu Group (601689.SH) is a Ningbo auto-parts leader applying precision manufacturing to humanoid actuators.

Actuator Auto Parts Listed Ningbo
拓普集团

Auto-parts giant into robot actuators

Company Overview

  • Tuopu is a leading supplier of suspension, interior and EV components, deeply tied to Tesla and Chinese EV makers.
  • It is developing precision actuators and components for humanoid robots, leveraging its manufacturing scale.
  • Tuopu is among the highest-profile incumbents in the humanoid supply chain.
