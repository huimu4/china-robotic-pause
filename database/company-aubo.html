AUBO Robotics

AUBO Robotics was among China's first collaborative-robot (cobot) companies, building safe, affordable arms for industry and the world's largest robotics-education ecosystem.

Cobot Education Industrial Beijing
遨博智能

China's collaborative-robot pioneer

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • AUBO launched its first cobot in 2015, becoming one of the very first Chinese companies to compete with Universal Robots in safe, human-collaborative arms.
  • Its i-series arms are widely used in 3C assembly, education, healthcare assist and light manufacturing.
  • AUBO also powers a massive robotics-education ecosystem, with its arms deployed in thousands of schools and training centers across China.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Self-developed joint modules integrating motors, reducers, encoders and torque sensors.
  • 6-DOF and 7-DOF arm variants.
  • Safety features: torque limiting, collision detection, hand-guided teaching.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • AUBO's joint modules are largely in-house, using domestic harmonic reducers from suppliers such as Leaderdrive.
  • Its scale in education helps amortize R&D across a broad installed base.
  • Component localization keeps AUBO arms price-competitive against Universal Robots and overseas rivals.

Competitor Comparison

Versus JAKA (节卡): AUBO leads in education and has longer history, while JAKA is stronger in industrial applications. Versus Universal Robots, AUBO wins on price and localization in China.
