EngineAI

EngineAI is the Shenzhen humanoid startup behind the T800, known for robots that run and tumble, backed by Xpeng Motors' founder.

Humanoid T800 Shenzhen
众擎机器人

The acrobatic humanoid from Shenzhen

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • EngineAI was founded by Zhao Tongyang, a serial humanoid entrepreneur, and quickly became known for robots that run, jump and recover from falls.
  • Its T800 and related humanoids target a balance of dynamic mobility and manipulation for research and commercial use.
  • The company has attracted major strategic backing, including from Xpeng Motors' founder ecosystem.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Self-developed high-torque joint actuators.
  • Proprietary gait and balance algorithms for dynamic running.
  • Vision + IMU perception for environment adaptation.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • EngineAI designs actuators in-house and sources reducers from domestic suppliers.
  • Shenzhen's component ecosystem keeps hardware costs competitive.
  • Rapid iteration funded by strong VC backing.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Unitree H1 (acrobatic): EngineAI competes on dynamic humanoid performance; Unitree has larger shipping volume. Versus AgiBot: EngineAI is more focused on dynamic mobility, less on dexterous manipulation.
