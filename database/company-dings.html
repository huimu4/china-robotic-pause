Company Overview

  • DINGS Motion (江苏鼎智智能控制科技, NEEQ: 873593) was founded in 2008 in Changzhou and listed on the Beijing Stock Exchange. It is a national "little giant" specialist in precision stepper motors and linear motion.
  • Core products include linear actuators, hybrid stepper motors, voice-coil motors, brushless DC motors, frameless torque motors, hollow-cup motors (6–14 mm) and joint actuator modules — spanning 4.5 N·m to 50 N·m.
  • The company has expanded from medical/IVD equipment into semiconductors, low-altitude flight and humanoid robotics; it received an "Agibot Excellent Partner Award" in March 2025, and its robot projects have moved from sample into batch production.
