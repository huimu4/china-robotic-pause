Frequently Asked Questions

What does X-Human do?

X-Human (凌度智能) is a Chinese home-robot company focused on Cleaning robots. The category is dominated by Chinese brands, which now ship most of the world's robot vacuums.

How does X-Human compete with Roborock, Ecovacs or Dreame?

The Chinese cleaning-robot market is fiercely competitive, with Roborock, Ecovacs and Dreame leading globally. X-Human differentiates on Cleaning robots, a segment where Chinese supply chains keep costs and feature cycles aggressive.
