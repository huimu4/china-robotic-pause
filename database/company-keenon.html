Keenon Robotics

Keenon Robotics is a Shanghai leader in delivery robots, deploying across restaurants, hotels and hospitals in China and overseas.

Delivery Service Hospital Shanghai

Shanghai's delivery-robot scale-up

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Keenon built one of China's largest deployed fleets of delivery robots for restaurants, hotels and hospitals.
  • Its robots handle food delivery, hospital logistics and hotel concierge services.
  • The company has expanded into Southeast Asia, Europe and other overseas markets.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Self-developed navigation and fleet scheduling.
  • Multi-modal sensing for crowded indoor environments.
  • Healthcare-grade delivery configurations.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Keenon integrates domestic motors, sensors and batteries.
  • Software and fleet management are key differentiators.
  • Scale in Chinese hospitality drives cost down.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Pudu: Keenon is a close domestic rival in delivery robots, with strong hospital and hotel presence, while Pudu leads in global restaurant deployment.
