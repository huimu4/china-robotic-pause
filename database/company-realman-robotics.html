Realman Robotics

Realman Robotics (睿尔曼) makes ultra-lightweight robot arms — the RM65 — plus teleoperation networks and data services for embodied AI.

Arm Lightweight Beijing
睿尔曼

Ultra-light arms & teleoperation for embodied AI

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Realman's RM65 is an ultra-light (7.2kg) 6-DOF arm with 5kg payload and ±0.05mm repeatability, popular in research and service robotics.
  • The company now also provides teleoperation data services and delivered China's first humanoid-robot data-training center.
  • Positioned as a 'global system-level infrastructure for physical AI'.

Actuators & Sensors

  • 7-DOF exoskeleton teleoperation with 100Hz.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Beijing R&D with Changzhou/Shenzhen manufacturing.
  • In-house joint modules and motion-control stack.

Competitor Comparison

Competes with Elephant Robotics (education) and domestic arm makers; differs by focusing on embodied-AI data infrastructure.
