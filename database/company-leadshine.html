Company Overview

What does Leadshine do?

Leadshine (雷赛智能) is a Chinese robotics company focused on Servo systems & motion control. It is part of China's fast-growing robotics ecosystem, which spans industrial, service and humanoid robots.

China's robotics sector is broad and competitive. Leadshine focuses on Servo systems & motion control, competing with domestic peers and international companies through cost, integration and speed.
