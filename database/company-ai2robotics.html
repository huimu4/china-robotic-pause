The Shenzhen humanoid startup built by a former XPeng and OPPO chief scientist — valued at ~RMB 20B and dubbed "China's Tesla Optimus."

Company Overview

  • AI²Robotics (智平方) was founded in April 2023 in Shenzhen by Guo Yandong, a Purdue PhD who previously served as a chief scientist at XPeng and OPPO and as a researcher at Microsoft's Redmond AI team.
  • The company targets "productivity-type" general humanoids — not demo-stage show robots — and pairs its hardware with the in-house AlphaBrain control stack and GOVLA vision-language-action foundation model.
  • It is often described by Chinese media as "China's Tesla Optimus" because of the founder's automotive-AI background and the company's vertical integration from "brain" to body.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Vertical integration of on-device compute and control stack, drawing on the founder's XPeng and OPPO "smart terminal" experience.
  • Humanoid hardware tuned for repeatable industrial productivity tasks rather than bipedal acrobatics.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Unitree and AgiBot: AI²Robotics leans more on the automotive-AI/smart-terminal playbook — on-device intelligence, consumer-electronics cost engineering — and positions itself closest to Tesla Optimus in product philosophy. Versus Galbot and X Square: it is hardware-led rather than pure model lab.
