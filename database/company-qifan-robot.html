Company Overview

  • Qifan Robot is a Guangzhou-based automation supplier focused on gantry robots, spraying lines and special-purpose machinery.
  • Its systems serve manufacturers that need overhead material handling, machine tending and surface-coating automation rather than standard six-axis arms.
  • Qifan is representative of China's mid-market automation integrators: engineering-driven, project-based, and tightly coupled to local manufacturing demand.

What Qifan Builds

Gantry robots — Cartesian systems with overhead X/Y/Z axes — are Qifan's core product line. They suit factories with large work envelopes and heavy payloads where a floor-mounted articulated arm is impractical. Typical jobs include machine loading, palletizing and transfer between process stations.

Spraying lines extend the offering into surface finishing: automated booths that coat components consistently, reducing labor cost and material waste versus manual spraying. Qifan integrates these as turnkey cells, covering the conveyor, robot, booth and controls.

Competitor Comparison

China's gantry and spraying automation market is fragmented. Qifan positions itself on special-purpose lines — spraying booths and turnkey handling cells — rather than competing with large general-purpose industrial robot brands such as SIASUN or Estun. Buyers typically shortlist Qifan for coating and material-handling projects that need custom integration.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Qifan Robot do?

Qifan Robot designs and builds gantry robots, spraying lines and special-purpose automation systems, mainly for Chinese manufacturing plants that need material handling, machine tending and surface coating automation.

What is a gantry robot?

A gantry robot is a Cartesian-coordinate robot that moves along overhead linear axes (X, Y and often Z). Because the drive system sits above the work area, gantry robots handle heavy loads over large footprints and are widely used in China for machine loading, palletizing, welding and spraying stations.
