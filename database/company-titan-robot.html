TiTaN Robot

TiTaN Robot is a Shanghai startup building humanoid robots and high-performance dexterous hands for embodied AI.

Humanoid Dexterous Shanghai
钛虎机器人

Dexterous hands & humanoid platforms

Company Overview

  • TiTaN Robot develops dexterous robotic hands and humanoid platforms, focusing on hand dexterity as the key to useful robots.
  • Its hands and robots serve research, embodied-AI companies and potential service applications.
  • The startup is part of Shanghai's growing humanoid supply chain.

Key Facts

Full NameTiTaN Robot (Shanghai)
Chinese Name钛虎机器人科技（上海）有限公司
Founded2022

Core ProductsDexterous hands, humanoid robots
StatusEmerging humanoid & hand maker

Actuators & Sensors

  • Multi-DOF dexterous hands with tactile capability.
  • Humanoid arm and platform integration.
  • High-bandwidth control for fine manipulation.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • TiTaN leverages Shanghai robotics hardware ecosystem.
  • Domestic sensors, motors and reducers.
  • Partners with embodied-AI software makers.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Inspire Robots and PaXini (hands): TiTaN competes with integrated hand + humanoid platforms rather than components only.
