Haozhi Mechanical

Haozhi Mechanical is a Guangzhou company producing harmonic reducers for robot joints alongside precision spindles for CNC machines.

Company Overview

  • Haozhi built its reputation on high-speed precision spindles for CNC and PCB machines, then expanded into harmonic reducers for robots.
  • Its reducers target industrial robots and growing humanoid demand.
  • The company is part of China's push to localize precision robot components.
