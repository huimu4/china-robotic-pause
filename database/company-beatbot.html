Beatbot

Beatbot (星迈创新) is a Suzhou startup building premium robotic pool cleaners — the AquaSense series — that challenge the category's global incumbents.

Pool Consumer Suzhou
星迈创新

High-end robotic pool cleaners with smart navigation

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Beatbot targets the premium end of the pool-robot market with multi-sensor navigation and smarter cleaning patterns.
  • Its AquaSense models compete directly on performance with top-tier US/EU brands while shipping from China.
  • A poster child of China's consumer-robotics export wave.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Brushless drives with vertical-wall & waterline climbing.
  • Sensor fusion for coverage mapping and obstacle avoidance.
  • High-efficiency filtration and long battery.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Suzhou supply chain for motors, pumps and electronics.
  • Premium brand marketing in North America & Europe.

Competitor Comparison

Rivals Aiper on price-to-performance and Maytronics/Dolphin on premium; Beatbot's edge is smart navigation at lower price.
