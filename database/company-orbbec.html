Orbbec

Orbbec is China's leading 3D-vision-sensor company (688322.SH), producing depth cameras and vision modules used in robotics, smart devices and industrial automation.

Company Overview

  • Orbbec develops structured-light, ToF and binocular depth-sensing technology, shipping modules into robotics, smartphones, smart door locks and more.
  • Its depth cameras are increasingly embedded in Chinese robots — AMRs, AGVs, cobots and humanoids — for obstacle avoidance, navigation and manipulation.
  • Orbbec has built a global presence competing with Intel RealSense and Apple-style depth solutions in the robotics and IoT markets.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Structured-light and ToF depth cameras.
  • Integrated 3D vision + IMU modules for robotics.
  • SDK support for ROS, PyTorch and common robot frameworks.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Orbbec designs its own ASIC and optical components, manufacturing through Chinese fabs and ODMs.
  • Its technology is a key piece of the Chinese robot sensor supply chain.
  • Competition includes foreign depth-sensing leaders, but Orbbec offers localization and cost advantages.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Intel RealSense: Orbbec competes on price and Chinese supply-chain integration. In robotics, its depth modules face competition from domestic LiDAR makers for different sensing needs (depth vs. lidar).
