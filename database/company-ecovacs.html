Ecovacs Robotics

Ecovacs turned China's first consumer robot company into a global robot-vacuum and home-cleaning leader, listed as 603486.SH.

Home Vacuum Listed Suzhou

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Ecovacs began as a vacuum-cleaner OEM in 1998 and launched China's first home robot in 2006, building the DEEBOT robot-vacuum line into a global brand.
  • It expanded into window-cleaning, commercial service robots and premium mopping robots, competing head-on with iRobot and Roborock.
  • Ecovacs is a prime example of China creating a global consumer-robotics category from scratch.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Self-developed navigation SLAM and cleaning-scheduling software.
  • LiDAR/vision-based navigation, mopping and auto-docking systems.
  • Commercial robot lines for malls, hotels and offices.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Ecovacs integrates Chinese supply chains for motors, sensors and batteries.
  • Its in-house software stack is the key differentiator against generic OEM rivals.
  • Scale in home robots funds R&D into commercial and humanoid-adjacent lines.

Competitor Comparison

Versus iRobot (Roomba): Ecovacs leads globally in premium robot mops and competes on price and features. Versus Roborock and Narwal, it is the older, broader home-robot brand.
