Databot Robotics

Databot Robotics (达闼机器人) builds cloud-connected robots powered by its HARIX cloud brain — from humanoid R&D to service robots.

Company Overview

  • Databot champions cloud robots: heavy AI runs in the HARIX cloud brain while robots stay light and cheap.
  • Its lineup spans humanoid R&D, patrol and cleaning robots.
  • Founded by veteran telecom executive Bill Huang (ex-China Mobile Research Institute).
