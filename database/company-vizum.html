Vizum

Vizum (伟景智能) is a Beijing company combining industrial vision modules with humanoid and companion robot platforms.

Company Overview

  • Vizum supplies 3D vision modules and vision-guided robot solutions to industry.
  • It also develops humanoid and companion robot platforms that showcase its sensing stack.
  • A bridge between computer vision and robotics in Beijing.
