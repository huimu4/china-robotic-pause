OrionStar

OrionStar, built by Cheetah Mobile and Xiaomi alum, makes AI-powered greeting, delivery and interaction robots for enterprises.

Service AI Beijing

AI service robots for interaction & delivery

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • OrionStar was created by Cheetah Mobile and Xiaomi alumni to build AI-native service robots for business settings.
  • Its robots provide greeting, guidance, delivery and interaction in shopping malls, banks, hotels and museums.
  • The company emphasizes an AI-first approach with voice, vision and large-model integration.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • OrionStar integrates Chinese supply chains and focuses on software/AI.
  • Cheetah Mobile and Xiaomi ecosystem provide distribution and tech synergy.
  • AI large-model integration is a growing differentiator.
