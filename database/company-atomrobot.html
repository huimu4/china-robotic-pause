Atomrobot

Atomrobot (阿童木机器人) builds high-speed Delta and SCARA robots from Tianjin, used in food, pharmaceutical and light-industry pick-and-place.

Delta SCARA Tianjin

High-speed Delta & SCARA robots

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Atomrobot specializes in parallel (Delta) and SCARA robots optimised for speed in light-industry lines.
  • Its machines handle sorting, packaging and assembly in food and pharmaceutical factories.
  • Tianjin University-linked engineering roots.

Key Facts

Actuators & Sensors

  • High-speed delta kinematics with lightweight carbon arms.
  • Integrated servos and controllers.
  • Vision-guided picking options.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Tianjin/Beijing manufacturing cluster.
  • Domestic servo and reducer sourcing.

Competitor Comparison

Competes with ESTUN, SIASUN and foreign Delta makers (ABB, Fanuc) on speed and price.
