DH Robotics (大寰机器人) makes electric parallel grippers and servo-driven end-effectors in Shenzhen, trusted by global arm makers.

Company Overview

  • DH Robotics specialises in electric grippers with precise force and position control for robot arms.
  • Its products are integrated by cobot and industrial arm makers globally.
  • A key end-effector supplier in China's robot ecosystem.
