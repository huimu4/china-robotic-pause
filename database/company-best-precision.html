Best Precision (300580.SZ) is a Wuxi auto-parts maker producing planetary roller screws for humanoid linear actuators.

Company Overview

  • Best Precision grew in precision automotive parts, building expertise in high-precision machining.
  • It has developed planetary roller screws, a critical linear-actuator component for humanoid robots.
  • The company is a representative of auto-parts suppliers pivoting to the humanoid boom.
