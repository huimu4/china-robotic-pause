Wisson

Wisson (万勋科技) develops soft, tendon-driven robotic arms — the Pliabot series — that give drones and ground robots safe manipulation ability.

Soft-robot Aerial Shenzhen
万勋科技

Soft arms that turn drones into workers

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Wisson's soft arms use tendon/air actuation instead of rigid joints, making them inherently safe and lightweight.
  • Its Pliabot arms mount on drones for aerial cleaning, inspection and picking — a category it helped create.
  • A standout of China's soft-robotics scene.
