Deep Robotics

Deep Robotics is one of the world's most deployed quadruped companies, selling the X-series robot dogs for industrial inspection, with a growing line of bipedal robots.

Quadruped Legged X20 Hangzhou
云深处科技

Hangzhou quadruped pioneer going global

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Founded by Zhejiang University robotics professor Zhu Qiuguo, Deep Robotics was the first Chinese company to field industrial-grade quadruped robots at scale.
  • Its X-series robot dogs (X20, X30, X50) perform power-station inspection, emergency response and oil-field patrol, with deployments across China, Southeast Asia and the Middle East.
  • In 2024-2025 Deep Robotics expanded into bipedal humanoid research with the Dr. E series, extending its legged-control expertise to two legs.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Self-developed high-torque joint actuators and drives.
  • Proprietary control algorithms for climbing stairs, slopes and unstructured terrain.
  • Perception stack combining LiDAR, stereo cameras and IMU.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Deep Robotics vertically integrates motor and controller design in Hangzhou, sourcing reducers and bearings from domestic suppliers.
  • Its scale in industrial legged robots gives it component-cost advantages most humanoid startups lack.
  • Manufacturing is largely in-house, enabling rapid iteration across the X and Dr. E platforms.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Unitree (quadruped line): Deep Robotics targets professional/industrial buyers with rugged X-series units, while Unitree pushes cost-competitive consumer and research models. In humanoids, Deep Robotics is a later entrant than Unitree or AgiBot.
