Topstar 拓斯达

Dongguan's first robot-maker on ChiNext. Started in injection-molding automation in 2007, listed in 2017, and in 2025 pivoted into embodied AI — launching the Xiao Tuo wheeled humanoid with Zhipu AI inside its own injection workshops. H1 2026 robot business grew 121.6%.

300607.SZ Founded 2007 Dongguan 12,000 robots/yr 15,000 customers

Why Topstar matters

Topstar is the rare Chinese robot company that is already profitable and already shipping — not a humanoid pre-revenue story. It built its business around the injection-molding shops of the Pearl River Delta, selling the plastic-molding machine itself plus the take-out robot, CNC cell and controls around it. That vertical integration is now its moat: when it decided to put a humanoid on the factory floor in 2025, it had 15,000 existing customers and its own injection workshops as a test bed.

In September 2025 Topstar co-founded Matrix Zhituo (矩阵智拓) with Beijing LLM lab Zhipu AI (智谱), and under that JV launched Xiao Tuo (小拓) — the first humanoid robot deployed inside a Chinese injection-molding factory. In 2026 Topstar was named to the Global Embodied AI Benchmark Top 100 list.

The Xiao Tuo bet: why wheeled, not bipedal

Unlike Tesla Optimus, Figure 02 or UBTech Walker S, Topstar's first humanoid is wheeled. The engineering logic is straightforward: inside a controlled factory floor, bipedal legs add cost, energy consumption and fall risk without adding value. A wheeled base with a 7-DOF dual-arm upper body can carry the same payload, run 8 hours on a swappable battery, and be deployed next to an injection press within days. Topstar's CEO Wu Fengli has framed this as "humanoids first earn their keep inside existing factories, not on public streets."

The brain comes from Zhipu AI's GLM series via the Matrix Zhituo JV — Topstar contributes the mechanical platform, factory scenarios and 15,000-customer distribution; Zhipu contributes the vision-language-action model. This mirrors the OpenAI + Figure AI structure, but on the Chinese supply chain.

Topstar is not the largest Chinese industrial-robot maker by revenue — Estun and Siasun are bigger. Its edge is vertical integration in plastics/3C and a profitable, cash-generating robot business that funds the humanoid bet without a capital raise.

Competitive position

Against ABB, FANUC and Yaskawa, Topstar prices its articulated robots 40–60% lower and offers same-week integration in the Pearl River Delta — a service no Japanese or European vendor can match for small-batch 3C and plastics shops. Against domestic peers (Estun, Siasun, Inovance, Effort), Topstar is differentiated by the injection-molding machine itself: it is the only Chinese robot maker that also sells the press the robot tends. That "machine + robot + CNC + controls" bundle is why its industrial-robot segment grew 121.6% in H1 2026 while 3C customers expanded orders.

Risks & watchpoints

  • 3C cyclicality — Topstar's robot growth is tightly tied to 3C (consumer electronics) capex; a slowdown hits orders quickly.
  • Humanoid revenue is still small — Xiao Tuo and Xingzai are pilot deployments, not material revenue yet. The H1 2026 profit jump is from industrial robots, not humanoids.
  • Competition from Inovance / Efort — Inovance (300124) is eating into the domestic servo-and-robot stack from the motion-control side.
  • Wheel vs biped — If the market converges on bipedal humanoids for factories, Topstar's wheeled-first bet may need a hardware rebuild.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Topstar (拓斯达) make?

Topstar builds industrial six-axis and SCARA robots, injection-molding machines (90–2,800t), CNC machines, and since 2025 the Xiao Tuo wheeled humanoid and Xingzai quadruped built with Zhipu AI. It shipped about 12,000 industrial robot units in 2025.

Is Topstar a Chinese humanoid robot company?

Yes, but via a JV. In September 2025 Topstar and Zhipu AI formed Matrix Zhituo (矩阵智拓), which launched the Xiao Tuo (小拓) wheeled humanoid — the first humanoid deployed in a Chinese injection-molding workshop. Topstar also sells the Xingzai (星仔) heavy-duty quadruped for inspection and firefighting.

Who founded Topstar?

Wu Fengli (吴丰礼), a PLA veteran who started at a Taiwanese-owned Dongguan factory in 2001 and founded Topstar in 2007 with 500,000 CNY. He took the company public on the Shenzhen ChiNext board on 9 February 2017 — the first Guangdong robot-maker listed on ChiNext.

How big is Topstar financially?

Full-year 2025 revenue was 2.51 billion CNY with net profit of 74 million CNY. H1 2026 revenue hit 1.29 billion CNY (+18.6% YoY) and net profit 104 million CNY (+263%). The industrial-robot & automation segment alone grew 121.6% to 707 million CNY in H1 2026.

How does Topstar compare with Estun, Siasun or ABB?

Topstar is smaller than Estun (002747.SZ) and Siasun (688017.SH) in pure robot revenue, but vertically unique: it builds its own injection-molding machines, which is exactly where its 15,000 factory customers buy robots. Against ABB/FANUC it competes on price (40–60% lower), local integration speed, and 3C/plastics verticals in the Pearl River Delta.

Is Xiao Tuo a full humanoid or a wheeled robot?

Xiao Tuo is a wheeled humanoid (轮式人形) with a 7-DOF dual-arm upper body — not a biped. Topstar's logic is that bipedal legs are unnecessary inside a controlled factory floor, so the wheeled base is cheaper, safer and faster to production.
