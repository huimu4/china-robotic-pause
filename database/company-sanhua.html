Sanhua Intelligent Controls (002050.SZ) is a Shaoxing components giant applying actuation and thermal tech to humanoid robots.

Company Overview

  • Sanhua is a global leader in thermal-management and precision components for HVAC and EVs.
  • It is developing actuators and precision components for humanoid robots, leveraging its mechatronics expertise.
  • Sanhua is a prominent example of a components giant pivoting into the robotics supply chain.
