Yarbo

Yarbo (汉阳科技) makes modular outdoor robots — a snow blower, lawn mower and leaf blower on one base — funded by global crowdfunding backers.

Yard Snow Blower Consumer
汉阳科技

Modular robots for the great outdoors

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Yarbo pioneered the modular outdoor-robot concept: one autonomous base that switches between snow-blowing, mowing and leaf-blowing modules.
  • Raised millions via Kickstarter and global DTC sales, shipping to snow-heavy North American markets.
  • Positioned as the 'outdoor companion' robot against winter chores.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Tracked/wheeled base with high-torque drives.
  • Modular quick-attach implements (snow, mow, leaf).
  • RTK + vision navigation for yard mapping.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Shenzhen design + Guangdong manufacturing.
  • Modular parts ecosystem for interchangeable tools.

Competitor Comparison

Faces emerging rivals in robotic mowers (e.g. EcoFlow, Mammotion) but holds a unique modular snow-blower niche.
