Kepler K2

Kepler K2 is a full-size humanoid from Shanghai's Kepler Robotics, targeting industrial deployment at competitive cost.

Kepler Robotics (开普勒)Product
Kepler Robotics (开普勒)

Kepler K2

Overview

  • Kepler builds full-size humanoids aimed at industrial and logistics tasks, with the K2 as its flagship.
  • The company emerged as one of the few Chinese startups targeting sub-US$30,000 full-size humanoids.
  • K2 is positioned to compete directly with Unitree G1 on price while offering a taller, heavier form factor.

Specifications

HeightApprox 175 cm
WeightApprox 80 kg
Degrees of FreedomFull-body humanoid DOF
MobilityBiped walking with industrial payload
TargetIndustrial, logistics, warehouse
Release Date2024
PricingTargeted sub-US$30,000 class

Actuators & Sensors

  • High-torque joint actuators for full-size payloads.
  • Modular limb design for serviceability.
  • Vision + IMU perception with industrial focus.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Kepler sources components across China's humanoid supply chain.
  • Beneficiary of Shanghai's embodied-AI industry cluster and funds.
  • Reducers and actuators from domestic suppliers including Leaderdrive.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Unitree G1, Kepler K2 is taller and heavier, closer in role to AgiBot A2 and EngineAI T800 — but targets a lower price than full-size rivals to compete with G1's volume play.
