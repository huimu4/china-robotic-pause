Galbot (Galaxy General)

Galbot is the Beijing startup building a wheeled humanoid (the Galbot G1) paired with its own Vision-Language-Action foundation models, betting that wheeled mobility reaches commercial utility faster than bipedal walking.

Humanoid Embodied AI G1 Beijing
银河通用

Wheeled humanoid robots + embodied foundation models

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Galbot is among the most prominent embodied-AI startups in China, founded in 2023 by researchers from top AI labs.
  • Its core bet: instead of chasing perfect bipedal walking, deploy a wheeled humanoid with a capable torso, arms and hands, and drive it with Vision-Language-Action (VLA) foundation models trained for manipulation.
  • The company has demonstrated commercial pilots in retail, logistics and service settings, positioning itself as a software-plus-hardware play rather than a pure robot maker.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Lightweight, high-DOF dual arms and dexterous hands optimized for manipulation over locomotion.
  • Wheeled base for stable, energy-efficient indoor navigation.
  • Depth + RGB camera rig, LiDAR, and wrist force-torque sensing.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Galbot outsources standard actuators and reducers to domestic suppliers and focuses in-house effort on the control stack and foundation models.
  • The arm joints use harmonic reducers sourced from Chinese makers including Leaderdrive and Hengtong.
  • Compute runs on domestic AI accelerator boards, with a cloud-side training pipeline.

Competitor Comparison

Versus bipedal humanoids (Unitree, AgiBot, Fourier): Galbot trades legs for a wheeled base and bet on manipulation + VLA models. This gives it lower cost and faster indoor deployment, at the cost of stair/terrain mobility.
