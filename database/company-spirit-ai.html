Spirit AI

Spirit AI is a Hangzhou startup building an LLM-powered embodied-AI brain aimed at giving robots human-like understanding and action.

Company Overview

  • Spirit AI applies large-language-model reasoning to robot control, aiming for natural, human-like task execution.
  • Its models target both manipulation and whole-body humanoid control.
  • Hangzhou's AI ecosystem (Alibaba-adjacent) supports its development.
