Frequently Asked Questions

What does Langyong Future do?

Langyong Future (浪涌未来) is a Chinese home-robot company focused on Underwater cleaning robots. The category is dominated by Chinese brands, which now ship most of the world's robot vacuums.

How does Langyong Future compete with Roborock, Ecovacs or Dreame?

The Chinese cleaning-robot market is fiercely competitive, with Roborock, Ecovacs and Dreame leading globally. Langyong Future differentiates on Underwater cleaning robots, a segment where Chinese supply chains keep costs and feature cycles aggressive.
