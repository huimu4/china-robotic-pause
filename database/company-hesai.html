Hesai Technology

Hesai Technology is one of the world's largest LiDAR suppliers, expanding from autonomous vehicles into robotics sensing.

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Hesai is a global LiDAR leader whose sensors equip autonomous vehicles, and increasingly robots.
  • Its solid-state and mechanical LiDAR provide high-resolution 3D perception for AMRs, delivery robots and humanoids.
  • Automotive-scale manufacturing drives down cost for robot applications.
