Roborock

Roborock went from a Xiaomi-ecosystem startup to one of the world's most valuable robot-vacuum companies, listed as 688169.SH.

Home Vacuum Listed Beijing
石头科技

From Xiaomi ecosystem to global premium brand

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Roborock was founded by ex-Google engineers and grew inside Xiaomi's ecosystem before building its own premium brand.
  • Its robot vacuums combine strong navigation, mopping and self-cleaning docks, competing at the premium end globally.
  • The company has become a bellwether for Chinese smart-home robotics and AI-driven hardware.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Self-developed LiDAR and 3D-structure-light navigation.
  • Robust mopping, self-empty and self-clean docking systems.
  • Advanced obstacle avoidance using vision + depth sensors.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Roborock designs hardware in-house and manufactures through premium Chinese OEM supply chains.
  • Strong R&D margin reinvestment keeps it ahead of generic rivals.
  • Global distribution (Europe, US, Asia) drives premium pricing power.
