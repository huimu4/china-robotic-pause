X Square Robot

X Square Robot is a Beijing embodied-AI company building a unified Vision-Language-Action foundation model to control many robot bodies.

Embodied AI VLA Beijing
自变量机器人

Unified VLA foundation model for robots

Company Overview

  • X Square Robot builds a general-purpose Vision-Language-Action model that can control different robot platforms from natural-language and visual input.
  • Its approach is model-first: a common brain rather than proprietary hardware.
  • The company targets general manipulation and task execution across robots.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Model-agnostic control across robot arms and humanoids.
  • VLA-based perception and action generation.
  • Sim-to-real training pipelines.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • X Square focuses on software and models, integrating with multiple hardware makers.
  • Beijing AI talent and compute resources.
  • Strong venture funding for model training.

Competitor Comparison

Versus hardware-first humanoid makers: X Square competes as a brain provider, like Figure's Helix or foreign VLA players, but China-first.
