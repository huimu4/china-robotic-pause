MileBot

MileBot (迈步机器人) develops lower-limb rehabilitation exoskeletons and robotic training systems that help patients regain walking.

Exoskeleton Rehab Shenzhen
迈步机器人

Robotic rehabilitation for walking recovery

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • MileBot builds wearable lower-limb exoskeletons and gait-training robots for hospitals and rehab centers.
  • Its systems assist stroke and spinal-cord patients in regaining mobility through guided, repeatable training.
  • Part of China's growing rehabilitation-robotics sector.
