EngineAI T800

EngineAI T800 is a full-size humanoid from Shenzhen's EngineAI — the showman robot known for dancing, football and a viral kick.

EngineAI T800

Official site ↗

Overview

  • T800 is the flagship of EngineAI's ladder of T800 (general), SA02 (commercial) and PM01 (research) humanoids.
  • It gained global attention for agility stunts — dancing, playing football and even kicking its own founder in a viral demo.
  • EngineAI is one of the fastest-scaling Chinese humanoid startups, founded in 2023 in Shenzhen.

Specifications

Actuators & Sensors

  • High-torque joint actuators tuned for dynamic agility.
  • Force-sensitive actuation for balance and impact resilience.
  • Vision + IMU perception for locomotion and interaction.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • EngineAI designs its own core actuators in Shenzhen.
  • Reducer and sensor supply draws on China's humanoid component ecosystem (Zhongding, Leaderdrive).
  • Beneficiary of Shenzhen's humanoid industry support policies.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Unitree G1, T800 is a full-size machine positioned for commercial and showcase roles rather than low-cost research volume. Its PM01 smaller sibling competes directly with G1's budget segment.
