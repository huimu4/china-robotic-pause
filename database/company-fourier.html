Fourier Intelligence

Fourier Intelligence is the Shanghai firm that moved from rehabilitation exoskeletons to the GR-1 and GR-2 full-size humanoids, one of the few Chinese companies shipping humanoid robots into commercial scenes.

Humanoid Rehab GR-2 Shanghai
傅利叶智能

Shanghai-based humanoid robot & rehab robotics leader

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Fourier Intelligence began in 2015 building rehabilitation robotics — exoskeletons and therapy devices for hospitals — and is one of China's most profitable robotics startups from that base.
  • In 2023 it shocked the industry by pivoting into full-size humanoid robots with the GR-1, quickly followed by the GR-2 in 2025. The GR series targets general-purpose manipulation and walking for commercial deployment.
  • Fourier's background in exoskeleton force control gave it unusually strong whole-body balance and torque control, a rare advantage among new humanoid entrants.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Self-developed FSA (Fourier Servo Actuator) series covering rotary and linear joint actuation.
  • Proprietary force-control stack inherited from rehab exoskeleton R&D.
  • Multi-sensor suite: depth cameras, IMU, joint torque sensing.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Fourier develops joint actuators in-house and sources precision reducers (harmonic and planetary) from domestic Chinese suppliers such as Leaderdrive (Green Harmonic).
  • The compute module leverages domestic SoC solutions, keeping the bill of materials largely local.
  • Rehab product revenue funds humanoid R&D, reducing reliance on external capital for near-term runway.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Unitree G1: Fourier GR-2 is heavier and built for manipulation over raw speed. Versus AgiBot A2: both target commercial deployment, but Fourier's force-control heritage gives it an edge in interaction-heavy tasks.
