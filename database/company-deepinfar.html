Deepinfar Ocean

Deepinfar Ocean (深之蓝) builds underwater ROVs for inspection and rescue plus consumer swim-propulsion products — the Black Shark and White Shark series.

Underwater ROV Tianjin
深之蓝

Underwater robots and swim propulsion from Tianjin

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Deepinfar covers both professional underwater inspection robots and consumer swim-assist devices.
  • Its White Shark is a leading consumer swim propulsion unit; Black Shark targets industrial ROV use.
  • Strong in rescue, aquaculture, and marine engineering applications.

Supply-Chain Analysis
