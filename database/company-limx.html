LimX Dynamics

LimX Dynamics is a Shenzhen humanoid startup that made a name with wheel-footed and full-size bipedal robots, backed by Alibaba.

Humanoid Wheel-Foot Shenzhen
逐际动力

Wheel-footed and full-size humanoids

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • LimX Dynamics was founded by robotics academics and built the CL-1, one of China's most-watched wheel-footed humanoids.
  • Its TRON platform targets full-size bipedal humanoids for industrial and general tasks.
  • The startup is a leading representative of the new wave of Chinese humanoid companies racing toward mass deployment.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Wheel-footed locomotion combining legs and wheels.
  • Full-size humanoid joint actuation.
  • Vision + proprioceptive sensing for balance and navigation.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • LimX designs core actuators in-house and sources reducers from domestic suppliers.
  • Shenzhen supply chain and strong academic robotics ties.
  • Alibaba and other strategic investors provide capital and ecosystem access.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Unitree (G1/H1): LimX differentiates on wheel-footed design and industrial focus. Versus Boston Dynamics heritage robots, it targets lower cost and China-first deployment.
