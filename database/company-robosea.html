ROBOSEA

ROBOSEA (博雅工道) builds bionic robotic fish and underwater robots in Beijing — bio-inspired platforms for monitoring, education and research.

Company Overview

  • ROBOSEA focuses on biomimetic underwater platforms — robotic fish that swim with realistic tail motion.
  • Applications span water monitoring, education, exhibitions and research.
  • A distinctive niche within China's underwater-robotics ecosystem.
