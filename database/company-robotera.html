RobotEra

RobotEra is a Beijing humanoid startup whose XBot platform pairs strong bipedal mobility with some of China's most advanced dexterous hands.

Humanoid Dexterous Hand Beijing
星动纪元

Humanoids with leading dexterous hands

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • RobotEra, founded by researchers from Tsinghua, builds the XBot humanoid platform emphasizing whole-body control and dexterous manipulation.
  • Its XHand dexterous hands are among the most advanced in China, enabling fine object handling.
  • The company collaborates with leading research institutions and has attracted strong funding.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Self-developed high-torque joint actuators and force-controlled hands.
  • Whole-body motion control for dynamic walking and manipulation.
  • Vision, tactile and proprioceptive sensing.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • RobotEra designs actuators and hands in-house, with reducers from domestic suppliers.
  • Tsinghua robotics research heritage drives algorithm advantage.
  • Component localization keeps cost competitive.

Competitor Comparison

Versus AgiBot (dexterous hands): RobotEra emphasizes research-grade whole-body control; AgiBot focuses on industrial scale-up. Verses Unitree: RobotEra is more manipulation-first than mobility-first.
