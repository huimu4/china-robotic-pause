Hypershell

Hypershell (极壳科技) builds AI-powered leg exoskeletons for hiking, travel and daily use — the first consumer exoskeleton certified by TÜV Rheinland and SGS.

Exoskeleton Consumer Shanghai
极壳科技

The world's first consumer outdoor exoskeleton

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Hypershell applies AI motion algorithms and high-torque motors to leg exoskeletons for outdoor enthusiasts.
  • Its X Series (from ~US$699) delivers ~40% more leg strength and ~30% less physical exertion, marketed worldwide via DTC and crowdfunding.
  • The company calls itself 'the world's only independently certified consumer exoskeleton brand'.

Actuators & Sensors

  • High-torque brushless motors with 1000W peak on X Ultra.
  • HyperIntuition AI motion algorithm with 0.31s response.
  • Titanium & carbon-fiber lightweight frame (from 2.5kg).

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Motors, sensors and structural parts sourced in China; final assembly in Shanghai/Guangdong.
  • Direct-to-consumer sales with global shipping.

Competitor Comparison

Competes with ULS Robotics and MileBot in industrial exoskeletons, but targets recreation/consumer where it has few direct rivals.
