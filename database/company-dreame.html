Dreame Technology

Dreame built its brand on high-speed brushless motors, applying them to premium cordless vacuums and robot vacuums that compete globally.

Home Motor Vacuum Suzhou
追觅科技

High-speed motors powering premium home robots

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Dreame was founded by Xiaomi-ecosystem alumni and built world-class high-speed brushless motors in-house.
  • Its robot vacuums, cordless vacuums and personal-care devices compete at the premium end across China, Europe and the US.
  • Dreame has also shown humanoid-robot prototypes, extending its motor and control technology toward embodied machines.

Actuators & Sensors

  • In-house high-speed brushless motors (over 100,000 RPM).
  • Navigation and auto-empty docking for robot vacuums.
  • Humanoid prototype with high-torque joint actuation.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Dreame vertically integrates motor design and manufacturing in Suzhou.
  • Its motor tech is a genuine moat against generic OEM rivals.
  • Global distribution and premium pricing fund R&D.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Roborock and Ecovacs: Dreame competes on motor technology, price-performance and aggressive feature updates in the premium vacuum segment.
