Laifual Drive

Laifual Drive is a Shaoxing harmonic-reducer maker supplying precision joints to Chinese robot and humanoid builders.

Reducer Harmonic Shaoxing
来福谐波

Harmonic reducers for robots & humanoids

Company Overview

  • Laifual builds harmonic reducers that are a core alternative to Leaderdrive (Greensharmonics) for Chinese robot makers.
  • Its reducers serve industrial robots, collaborative arms and growing humanoid programs.
  • The company has expanded into integrated actuator modules for humanoid joints.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Harmonic reducers across sizes and ratios.
  • Integrated joint-actuator modules.
  • Precision gear components for robot arms.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Laifual masters flexspline and gear manufacturing in-house.
  • Zhejiang manufacturing ecosystem supports cost and scale.
  • Supplies to leading Chinese cobot and humanoid makers.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Leaderdrive (harmonic leader): Laifual is the main domestic challenger on price and supply, while Leaderdrive leads volume and brand.
