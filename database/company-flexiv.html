Flexiv

Flexiv builds force-controlled robot arms (Rizon series) that sense and adapt to contact, enabling tasks that fixed-motion robots cannot do.

Force Control Arms Shanghai
非夕科技

Force-controlled arms for adaptive manufacturing

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Flexiv was founded by Stanford robotics PhDs and built the Rizon series — 7-DOF arms with whole-arm force sensing.
  • Its arms handle tasks requiring contact compliance: sanding, polishing, assembling flexible parts and precise insertion.
  • Force control is positioned as the bridge between fixed industrial robots and human-like embodied manipulation.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Whole-arm joint torque sensing for safe contact.
  • 7-DOF kinematically dexterous arms.
  • Adaptive control for unstructured tasks.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Flexiv develops its own actuators and control stack in Shanghai.
  • Reducers and electronics from domestic suppliers.
  • Competes with high-end force-control rivals (Agile Robots, Franka).

Competitor Comparison

Versus Agile Robots (force-controlled): Flexiv is a close domestic rival with strong adaptive-manufacturing focus. Versus Franka Emika, Flexiv offers lower cost and Chinese supply chain.
