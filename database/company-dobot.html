Dobot Robotics

Dobot made desktop robot arms accessible to schools and makers worldwide, then grew into a full cobot company and one of the few robot makers listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange (02432.HK).

Cobot Desktop Listed Shenzhen
越疆科技

From desktop arms to Hong Kong's cobot listing

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • Dobot gained global fame with the Magician desktop arm, a low-cost 4-axis arm used in education, research and light hobby projects worldwide.
  • It expanded into professional CR-series collaborative arms for industrial and lab use, and listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in 2023.
  • Dobot's strength is in democratizing robotics — making arms cheap enough for classrooms and small businesses.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Cost-optimized joint modules for desktop and light-industrial loads.
  • 4-axis (Magician) and 6-axis (CR) families.
  • Vision kits and gripper ecosystem for education and automation.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • Dobot leverages Shenzhen's component ecosystem for motors and controllers at aggressive cost.
  • Its global education distribution is a key sales channel.
  • The HKEX listing gives it access to international capital for cobot and future humanoid R&D.

Competitor Comparison

Versus AUBO and JAKA: Dobot leads in education/desktop segment and is publicly listed, while AUBO/JAKA are stronger in industrial deployments. Its low-cost arms face competition from 3D-printed hobby arms but win on ecosystem.
