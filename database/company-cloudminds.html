CloudMinds

CloudMinds is a Beijing company pioneering cloud-brained humanoid robots (Ginger) with intelligence running in the cloud.

Humanoid Cloud Beijing
达闼机器人

Cloud-brained humanoid robots

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • CloudMinds was founded by former China Mobile CTO Huang Xiaoqing on the radical idea of running robot intelligence in the cloud.
  • Its Ginger humanoid serves in hotels, hospitals, banks and exhibitions, with thin client + cloud brain architecture.
  • The company has shipped humanoid robots at scale and built a robot cloud platform.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Cloud-brain (HARI) + robot client architecture.
  • Humanoid Ginger with arms, hands and mobile base.
  • Vision and voice AI streamed from the cloud.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • CloudMinds manufactures in China and sells globally.
  • The cloud-brain model reduces on-device compute cost.
  • Smart-device telecom heritage informs its connectivity approach.

Competitor Comparison

Versus UBTECH (service humanoids): CloudMinds differentiates on the cloud-brain architecture. Versus full-size industrial humanoids, it targets commercial service use cases.
