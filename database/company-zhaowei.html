ZHAOWEI (兆威机电)

The Shenzhen micro-drive specialist that shipped China's first commercial high-DOF dexterous hand — the ZWHAND series with up to 20 active DOF.

Dexterous Hand Micro Drive Shenzhen
兆威机电

Micro gearboxes + micro motors + controls → ZWHAND dexterous hand

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • ZHAOWEI (深圳市兆威机电, SZSE: 003021; A+H listing on HKEX since March 2026) was founded in 2001 and is a global leader in micro drive systems — gearboxes smaller than Φ6 mm at scale.
  • It was the first Chinese company to commercialize a high-DOF dexterous hand. Its ZWHAND series (A17, B20, B06) offers 17 active DOF expandable to 20, with each finger-joint integrating a coreless motor, precision planetary gearbox and sensors.
  • The company plans an RMB 800M dexterous-hand industrial park and positions the hand as its second growth curve alongside smart-car and consumer-medical micro drives.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Each finger-joint integrates a coreless motor, precision planetary gearbox and sensors into a single micro-drive module, enabling independent per-joint control.
  • The company's historical edge is high-precision micro gearboxes at production volume — the same capability now packed into dexterous hands.

Competitor Comparison

Versus Shadow Hand (UK), Wonik (Korea) and Tesla Optimus hand: ZHAOWEI competes on Chinese cost and vertical integration of micro gearboxes. Versus in-house hands from Galbot/Agibot: ZHAOWEI is a component vendor selling hands to multiple humanoid OEMs, not a robot maker itself.
