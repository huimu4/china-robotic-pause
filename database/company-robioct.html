Frequently Asked Questions

What does RoboCT do?

RoboCT (程天科技) is a Chinese company focused on Exoskeletons in the medical and rehabilitation robotics space, a sector where China is investing heavily.

How does RoboCT compare with Intuitive Surgical or global rehab-robot makers?

Chinese medical-robotics players such as RoboCT are developing alternatives to established systems like Intuitive's da Vinci. RoboCT focuses on Exoskeletons, with domestic approval pipelines and cost advantages.

Where is RoboCT based?

RoboCT (程天科技) is headquartered in Hangzhou, China, China.
