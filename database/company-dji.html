DJI

DJI is the Shenzhen company that created the consumer drone market and dominates it, now extending into industrial and agricultural robotics.

Drone Aerial Global Shenzhen
大疆创新

The company that built the global drone market

Official site ↗

Company Overview

  • DJI turned drone technology from a hobby into a global industry, holding a dominant share of consumer and enterprise drones.
  • Its agricultural drones (Agras) and enterprise platforms serve farming, inspection and public-safety robotics.
  • DJI's RoboMaster program educates a new generation of Chinese robotics engineers, reinforcing the talent pipeline.

Actuators & Sensors

  • Self-developed flight controllers, gimbals, motors and vision systems.
  • High-precision positioning and obstacle avoidance.
  • Agricultural spray and inspection payload systems.

Supply-Chain Analysis

  • DJI vertically integrates most core hardware in-house in Shenzhen.
  • Global supply chain with heavy in-house manufacturing.
  • RoboMaster and education ecosystem build future talent.

Competitor Comparison

Versus global drone rivals (GoPro, Parrot, Autel): DJI dominates on technology, price and ecosystem. It faces geopolitical restrictions in some markets but remains the category leader.
