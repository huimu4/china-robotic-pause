Huayan Robotics

Huayan Robotics (华沿机器人) is a Foshan maker of collaborative robot arms serving light industry, education and research.

Company Overview

  • Huayan builds collaborative arms for cost-sensitive automation in the Pearl River Delta.
  • Serves education, small workshops and light assembly.
  • Benefits from Foshan's manufacturing ecosystem.
