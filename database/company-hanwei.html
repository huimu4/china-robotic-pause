Hanwei Technology

Hanwei Technology (300007.SZ) is a Zhengzhou sensor maker extending its flexible-sensor expertise to robot skin and tactile sensing.

Company Overview

  • Hanwei is a leading Chinese sensor group, strongest in gas sensors and flexible sensing.
  • Its flexible-sensor technology is being developed for robot skin and tactile sensing applications.
  • Hanwei represents the sensing-supplier angle of the humanoid boom.
