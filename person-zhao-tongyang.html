Zhao Tongyang

The man kicked by his own robot — he restarted from scratch in 2023 with barely a year of living expenses.

赵同阳

Founder, EngineAI

Founder of EngineAI (众擎) · T800 humanoid

EngineAIThe showmanT800Shenzhen

Who he is

Zhao Tongyang is the man kicked by his own robot. A veteran of the industry, he bet a decade on robotics in 2016, sold his quadruped startup to XPeng in 2020, then restarted from scratch in 2023 with what he later said was barely a year of his kid's school fees in reserve.

The restart worked. His new company, EngineAI, hit a 10-billion-yuan valuation and is heading for a Hong Kong IPO. Its flagship T800 made headlines around the world by dancing, playing football and famously knocking its founder across a showroom with a kick.

Why he matters

Zhao is the industry's showman — but the viral moments sit on a serious product ladder: T800 for general use, SA02 for commercial, PM01 for research. His career arc, from selling his first startup to rebounding into a 10-billion-yuan valuation, is a reminder that the humanoid boom rewards endurance as much as brilliance.

Key facts

  • 2023: Restarted EngineAI from scratch with limited funds.
  • Valuation: 10 billion yuan; Hong Kong IPO in progress.
  • Viral: T800 knocked him across a showroom with a kick.
