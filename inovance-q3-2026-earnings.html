Inovance Q3 2026 Earnings: Automation Hits +33%, but Profit and Cash Tell a Second Story

On October 26–27, Shenzhen Inovance Technology (300124.SZ) filed its Q3 2026 report. The headline — revenue up nearly 20% — looks like another smooth quarter for China's biggest domestic industrial-automation vendor. Dig one layer down and the picture splits: general automation is booming on AI capex, while margins, cash flow and the NEV-powertrain business are all soft. Here is the full read, built only on the official filing and verified reporting.

By China Robotics Insider · Oct 28, 2026 · Last updated: Oct 28, 2026 · 9 min read
Share on X LinkedIn
The read · TL;DR
Inovance's nine-month revenue reached ¥37.96B (+19.9%), but net profit crept up only +3.4% to ¥4.40B and recurring profit was essentially flat at ¥3.91B (+0.5%). The growth engine is general automation (+33% to ¥15.9B) — servo ¥6.9B, inverters ¥4.8B, PLC/HMI ¥1.9B — pulled by AI data-center, optical-module, PCB and semiconductor-equipment capex. NEV powertrain grew just +8% to ¥15.5B, and the emerging robot/digital-energy line grew +88% to ¥2.1B. The red flags: gross margin down ~1.0 pt to 28.2%, operating cash flow down 77% to ¥0.9B, and inventory up 54% on strategic stockpiling. Q3's headline +23.6% net profit was flattered by ¥419M of investment fair-value gains; recurring Q3 profit actually fell 5.4%. Editorial research, not investment advice.

Inovance is the closest thing China has to a domestic Siemens: its servo systems, variable-frequency drives and small PLCs already lead Chinese brands in share, and it is now pushing into large PLCs, industrial robots and — its newest growth story — "industrial physical AI." The Q3 report is therefore read as a proxy for the whole Chinese automation and robotics supply chain. The short version: demand is real and broadening, but the profit and cash conversion that investors usually reward is lagging the top line.

The Q3 divergence: why net profit rose 24% but the operating profit fell

Q3 standalone revenue was ¥13.28B (+19.1% YoY, −8.6% QoQ). Reported net profit was ¥1.59B (+23.6%) — stronger than the nine-month trend. But recurring (扣非) net profit was ¥1.15B, down 5.4% YoY. The ¥437M gap between the two lines is almost entirely ¥419M of fair-value gains on financial assets and liabilities (largely mark-ups on equity investments), not operating performance.

This matters because it is the second consecutive quarter where headline profit overstates the underlying business. Strip out the investment mark-to-market and the Q3 industrial-automation recovery is not yet dropping to the bottom line — price competition in NEV parts, higher R&D (¥3.43B in nine months, ~9% of revenue) and a ¥542M inventory impairment (up from ¥232M a year earlier) are eating the gains.

Management attributes the 33% general-automation surge to three forces: AI supply-chain capex (data-center build-out pulling optical-module, PCB and semiconductor-equipment orders), a recovery in legacy downstream industries (lithium battery, machine tools, auto manufacturing, logistics, consumer electronics), and export follow-along as Chinese machine builders expand overseas. By H1 2026 Inovance held ~6.7% of China's mid-large PLC market and ~14.6% of the small-PLC market — both No.1 among domestic brands, still far behind Siemens domestically but closing the import-substitution gap. A functional-safety large PLC for continuous-process lines is now in customer trials at mining, port and municipal sites.

The NEV powertrain line (its United Power / joint-venture business) grew only 8% at ¥15.5B — a familiar story of brutal EV price competition compressing margins. Notably, management's own new equity-incentive targets exclude this business, which tells you where internal growth expectations sit.

The Physical AI pivot: InoBot W1 and the welding agents

Robotics has become Inovance's narrative, and the quarter delivered concrete product evidence rather than just roadmaps:

  • September: "Industrial Physical AI" platform — management's thesis is that consumer AI can afford mistakes, but factory AI cannot. Rather than a generic LLM or pure motion-imitation, Inovance is stacking "4+ agents" on a common control foundation, using its 70+ years of accumulated process know-how and current/vibration/vision data.
  • September 20: AI welding solution — four agents handle seam recognition, trajectory planning, process-parameter generation and weld inspection; 5 minutes to generate a weld path and first-article commissioning cut to 30 minutes.
  • October 13 (CIIS): InoBot W1 — a wheeled embodied robot, fully liquid-cooled, IP54, hot-swappable battery, demoed auto-loading/unloading into an automotive welding cell; the demo cell showed >20% higher output per hour, with metallurgy scrap rate down 3–8 points and energy-ops revenue up 3–8 points in pilot claims.

The emerging segment (robots + digital energy) at ~¥2.1B is still small (~5.5% of revenue), but the 88% growth rate and the explicit "Android of industrial physical AI" positioning — supplying the platform, not the last-mile vertical know-how — is the 2027 growth option the market is starting to price.

The balance sheet: stockpiling against what?

The numbers investors should watch more than the P&L this quarter:

  • Inventory ¥12.46B, +54.2% from year-start — deliberate strategic stockpiling to secure delivery, but paired with ¥542M inventory write-downs.
  • Receivables ¥13.33B, +¥1.82B year-to-date, plus ¥109M credit impairment.
  • Operating cash flow ¥0.90B vs ¥3.93B a year earlier — procurement cash out the door (¥20.2B, up from ¥14.6B) is running well ahead of collections.
  • Construction in progress ¥1.94B, +73.6% — new NEV-component capacity, R&D centers, Xi'an and Dalian sites.

Management frames this as growth-stage working-capital investment. The bull read: they are stocking parts to win the AI capex upcycle and delivery will convert the cash back in Q4. The bear read: receivables and inventory are building ahead of a demand cycle that may not hold. Q4 working-capital trends will settle it.

Frequently asked questions

What were Inovance's Q3 2026 results?

Nine-month revenue was 37.96 billion yuan (+19.88%), net profit attributable to shareholders 4.398 billion yuan (+3.38%), and recurring net profit 3.907 billion yuan (+0.49%). Q3 standalone revenue was 13.28 billion yuan (+19.08%) with net profit of 1.589 billion yuan (+23.55%), but recurring net profit fell 5.37% to 1.151 billion yuan.

Why is Inovance's profit growing slower than its revenue?

Blended gross margin fell about 1.03 percentage points to 28.24%, and net margin slipped about 1.85 points to 11.59%. Reasons include price pressure in NEV powertrain, higher R&D spending (~9% of revenue), and rising inventory and credit impairment charges. Q3's headline net profit was also boosted by about 419 million yuan of fair-value gains on equity investments rather than operations.

Which segment is growing fastest?

General-purpose automation grew about 33% to roughly 15.9 billion yuan, driven by AI data-center, optical-module, PCB and semiconductor-equipment demand. The emerging smart-robot and digital-energy business grew about 88% to 2.1 billion yuan. NEV powertrain grew only about 8% to 15.5 billion yuan.

What is Inovance doing in robots and physical AI?

In September it launched an "industrial physical AI" agent platform, followed by an AI welding solution (5-minute weld-path generation) and the wheeled InoBot W1 embodied robot shown at CIIS on October 13, which demoed automated loading of a welding cell at over 20% higher output. The company positions itself as the platform layer, not the vertical application layer.

Is Inovance's cash flow a problem?

Operating cash flow fell 77% to 895 million yuan in the nine months as inventory rose 54% to 12.46 billion yuan and receivables grew to 13.33 billion yuan. Management calls this growth-stage working capital; Q4 collections and inventory turnover are the numbers to verify the story.
