China's Robot Funding Heat Map 2026: RMB 61B in Four Months, From Humanoid Giants to Data Farms

The money arrived before the robots did. In Q1 2026 alone China's robot sector logged 200+ disclosed deals totaling over RMB 30B; by end-April the tally was 332 deals and RMB 61.3B, with humanoid robots taking 55 deals and RMB 25.9B of it — and 41 humanoid-related deals in April alone (Morgan Stanley), as 2026 global humanoid venture funding passed the whole of 2025. Then the money moved up the chain: from humanoid integrators to dexterous hands, joints, chips and — fastest of all — training-data companies. This is the heat map.

TL;DR
Scale: Q1 2026 = 200+ deals / RMB 30B+ (IT Juzi); Jan-Apr = 332 deals / RMB 61.3B, humanoid slice 55 deals / RMB 25.9B (36Kr); April = 41 humanoid deals (Morgan Stanley). Giants: Tashizhihang's Pre-A RMB 3B+ (record), Galbot RMB 2.5B at a RMB 20B valuation (National IC Fund III, Sinopec, SAIC…), Qianxun RMB 3B in 30 days (Lei Jun & Jack Ma-linked lead; >RMB 10B valuation), Xingdong Era RMB 2.5B+, Kunlunhang several billion, HX Intelligent RMB 1.7B (chips). Migration: data companies took three ~US$100M+ rounds on the same day (Nov 2: Jianzhi, GenRobot, RoboScience); parts (Linjiedian hands — Tencent+Baidu; Yita Dynamics joints) and chips (D-Robotics US$120M B1 with Didi/Meituan) heated up. Companion/home: Euler raised four rounds in six months (Huawei embodied-AI No.1 employee), CyboPal closed TCL/SCGC strategic round after 1,000+ preorders of its RMB 12,799 desktop robot. Liquidity test: Unitree's SSE STAR IPO (RMB 4.2B) filed March; 20+ firms plan listings. Watch item: MIIT counted 140+ humanoid integrators — a washout is widely expected.

1. The numbers: a sector that out-raised the whole of 2025 by April

Three independent tallies agree the scale. IT Juzi/Chinaventure: Q1 2026 saw over 200 disclosed robot financing events totaling more than RMB 30B — an average of more than two robot companies funded per day. 36Kr: in the first four months, 332 deals and RMB 61.3B, of which humanoid robots took 55 deals and RMB 25.9B. Morgan Stanley (via Sina Finance): 2026 global humanoid venture funding has already surpassed the whole of 2025, with Asia contributing ~46% of capital — and China recorded 41 humanoid-related financing cases in April 2026 alone, versus 16 in April of last year. Q1 humanoid full-chain deals exceeded 100, with the single largest round at RMB 2.5B and 15 deals at RMB 1B or above.

2. The giants: record rounds and the valuation ladder

  • Tashizhihang (它石智航) — RMB 3B+ Pre-A, Apr 2026: the largest single round in the sector's history; Hillhouse and Sequoia China led. Huawei/Baidu management pedigree.
  • Galbot (银河通用) — RMB 2.5B, Mar 2026: led by National IC Fund III with Sinopec, CITIC, Bank of China, SAIC Financial, SMIC-affiliated and Yizhuang state capital; valuation ~RMB 20B, breaking its own single-round record from Dec 2025.
  • Qianxun (千寻智能) — ~RMB 3B in 30 days, Feb+Apr 2026: nearly RMB 2B in Feb (Yunfeng, Ge Weidong's Chaos, Synstellation, TCL, Inovance-chairman family office, Chongqing/SH funds), then RMB 1B in Apr co-led by Shunwei (Lei Jun) and Yunfeng (Jack Ma) — a rare "Lei Jun + Jack Ma" co-lead; valuation crossed RMB 10B; JD-led RMB 600M Pre-A+ in Jul 2025 and a 2026-2029 strategic deal with JD retail.
  • Xingdong Era (星动纪元) — RMB 2.5B+ across 2026: US$200M+ in Apr; RMB 1B in Jul led by Chengtong Fund.
  • Kunlunhang (昆仑行) — several billion in three rounds, Jun 2026: Gaorong, Hillhouse, CAS Star, Zhongding, Huaye, Sinovation, Xinshin, Jianfa; founded Mar 2026 by ex-Huawei/Alibaba/New Hope and ex-Li Auto executives; world-model-first (KWM).
  • HX Intelligent (辉羲智能) — ~RMB 1.7B in two rounds, Oct 2026: Beijing AI Industry Fund led; >RMB 2.5B cumulative equity raised in 2026 for embodied compute chips; earlier backed by Xiaomi-led US$50M seed.

3. The migration: from bodies to hands, chips — and data

The most instructive signal of the year is where fresh money went after the integrator wave:

  • Core parts. Dexterous hands are the chokepoint: Linjiedian (临界点), AgiBot's spun-out hand maker, took rounds in Feb (internet-giant-led, Yunfeng) and May 2026 (Tencent + Baidu entered); Yita Dynamics (伊塔动力) raised a RMB 100M+ seed double in Oct (Matrix, SenseTime, CCB International, Galaxy General, Fourier); joint/hand modules became the new category (Jiben Zhineng's ~US$10M, Zaihong etc.).
  • Chips. D-Robotics (地瓜机器人) raised US$120M B1 in Mar with Didi and Meituan-Longzhu among the strategic backers (Horizon Robotics spin-out, 5-560 TOPS edge compute); HX Intelligent's RMB 1.7B targets embodied-compute silicon.
  • Data — the hottest lane. On Nov 2, three ~US$100M-class rounds landed on the same day for "no-body" data companies: Jianzhi (简智, US$100M A+), GenRobot (US$100M) and RoboScience (several hundred million RMB) — a structural read that bodies are commoditizing and training data is the scarce asset. The lane also includes Noritar (Noitom lineage, Pre-A+++), Xingyi (Tsinghua-incubated ego data), Lingke (Ling Shen), Maxinsights/Haiyu (US$30M seed) and Womai (data from cleaning platforms).

4. Companion and home: the quiet billion-yuan lane

Alongside humanoids, the "home and companion" thesis drew real money in 2026:

  • Euler (欧拉万象) — founded Mar 2026 by Zhou Shunbo, Huawei's No.1 embodied-AI employee (built Huawei's largest embodied team, led CloudRobo) — raised four rounds in six months (Hillhouse, Wuyuan, CMC Capital, Baidu Ventures, then Oct's RMB 100M+ angel+ with Chengyuanxiang, Xineng, Xizhou International, Topband, Hongyi). Thesis: "raisable" home robots that learn from users; RoboTwin 2.0 SOTA; first home deployments planned Q1 2027.
  • CyboPal (细胞壁) — Tsinghua PhD Peng Tianfang's desktop large-screen robot with 6-DoF arm (¥12,799 crowdfunding price); 1,000+ preorders / RMB 10M+ in a month; RMB 100M+ round led by Dongfang Fuhai (Sep), then a strategic round by TCL Industrial and Shenzhen Capital Group (Oct). A new hardware category betting that the desk — not the body — is where the personal agent lives.
  • Others: Youliqi (优理奇, hotel wheeled dual-arm; ~RMB 1B cumulative), Lingyan (灵颜, bionic emotional faces for companion robots), cell-level and desktop players (Alibaba's ¥899 QwenNote Eva desktop robot as the floor of the price curve).

5. Who is writing the checks

The 2026 check-writers form three distinct pools. Internet/platform giants: Tencent and Baidu (Linjiedian), ByteDance (Zibianliang RMB 1B A++, plus Moonshot/Unitree ties), Alibaba & Meituan (Zibianliang, D-Robotics), JD (Qianxun), Xiaomi (D-Robotics, Qianxun early), Huawei's Hubble (Shenshi tactile sensors), Ant (Datong Xiaoda, Physis). Industrial capital: SAIC, TCL, Topband, Tuopu, Inovance-chairman family office (Minghui Zhiyuan), Keli Sensing, Wingtech — buying a position in the next manufacturing cycle. State capital: National IC Fund III, Beijing/Shanghai AI industry funds, Shenzhen Capital, Yizhuang, regional mother funds — the largest pool, and the one most likely to keep funding through a downturn.

6. The exit test: IPOs and the washout

The sector's liquidity question is moving from the private to the public market. Unitree's SSE STAR Market IPO application (filed Mar 20, 2026, ~RMB 4.2B planned) is the bellwether; 20+ embodied-AI companies have declared listing plans; Shenzhen alone produced four "RMB 10B-valuation unicorns" in 2026 (Zibianliang, Zhiping, Paxi, EngineAI). The counter-signal: MIIT counted 140+ humanoid integrators in 2025, and industry consensus is that the field supports a fraction of that. The next filter — repeat orders, stable uptime, service cost — will separate the 2027 winners; the data lane's Nov spike suggests early positioning for exactly that reckoning.

Frequently asked questions

How much did China's robot sector raise in 2026?

Q1: 200+ deals, RMB 30B+; Jan-Apr: 332 deals, RMB 61.3B (humanoid: 55 deals / RMB 25.9B); April alone: 41 humanoid deals (Morgan Stanley). 2026 global humanoid VC already exceeds the full-year 2025 total, ~46% from Asia.

What were the biggest rounds?

Tashizhihang's RMB 3B+ Pre-A (record); Galbot RMB 2.5B at ~RMB 20B valuation; Qianxun ~RMB 3B in 30 days (Lei Jun + Jack Ma-linked leads); Xingdong Era RMB 2.5B+; Kunlunhang several billion; HX Intelligent ~RMB 1.7B for chips.

Where did 2026 funding concentrate beyond humanoid integrators?

Core parts (hands — Linjiedian; joints — Yita), chips (D-Robotics US$120M, HX RMB 1.7B), and especially data companies — Jianzhi/GenRobot/RoboScience each ~US$100M+ on the same day in Nov; also Noritar, Xingyi, Maxinsights.

What are the risks in this funding cycle?

140+ humanoid integrators (MIIT) vs a market that supports far fewer — a washout is widely expected. Much volume is pre-revenue; the next filter is repeat orders, uptime and service costs. Unitree's RMB 4.2B STAR IPO filing (Mar 2026) is the sector's liquidity test.
